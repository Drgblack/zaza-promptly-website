## SEO Optimization Guidelines

### On-Page SEO Checklist:
- ✅ Target keyword in title (front-loaded)
- ✅ Target keyword in first 100 words
- ✅ H1 includes primary keyword naturally  
- ✅ H2s include secondary keywords
- ✅ Internal links to related content (3-5 per post)
- ✅ External links to authority sources (1-2 per post)
- ✅ Image alt text includes relevant keywords
- ✅ Meta description compelling and keyword-rich (150-160 chars)

### Content Requirements:
- **Length:** 2,000-3,500 words for comprehensive coverage
- **Readability:** Grade 8-10 reading level for accessibility  
- **Structure:** Clear headings, bullet points, scannable format
- **Authority:** Include statistics, research, expert quotes
- **Actionability:** Practical tips, step-by-step guides
- **Visuals:** Screenshots, diagrams, infographics where relevant

### Internal Linking Strategy:
Each blog post should link to:
1. **Products page** - main conversion page
2. **FAQ page** - authority and trust building  
3. **Relevant persona pages** - targeted conversion
4. **Related blog posts** - content depth and engagement
5. **Free resources** - lead generation and value

### Featured Snippet Optimization:
- **Question format:** "What is..." "How to..." "Why is..."
- **List format:** Numbered or bulleted practical steps
- **Definition format:** Clear, concise explanations
- **Comparison format:** "X vs Y" structured comparisons

### Conversion Integration:
Each post should include:
- **Above-fold CTA:** Free trial or demo
- **Mid-content CTA:** Relevant resource or tool
- **End-of-post CTA:** Main conversion (pricing/signup)
- **Sidebar CTAs:** Newsletter, free resources
- **Content upgrades:** Downloadable guides, templates
