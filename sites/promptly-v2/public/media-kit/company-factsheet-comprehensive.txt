ZAZA PROMPTLY COMPANY FACTSHEET
===================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
=================

Zaza Promptly is a revolutionary AI-powered communication assistant specifically designed for educators worldwide. Founded in 2023 by Dr. Greg Blackburn, an educator-turned-entrepreneur with over 20 years of experience in education and learning development, the company addresses one of the most significant challenges facing teachers today: the overwhelming administrative burden that takes time away from actual teaching.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: empower educators with AI tools that save time, improve communication, and enhance student learning. We believe every teacher deserves time back, and we build AI that truly understands the classroom environment.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
=================

ZAZA PROMPTLY PLATFORM
-----------------------
Zaza Promptly is an AI-powered communication assistant that revolutionizes how teachers handle administrative communication tasks. Unlike generic AI tools, our platform is specifically designed for educational contexts, understanding the nuances of teacher-parent communication, student feedback, and professional educational discourse.

CORE PRODUCT FEATURES
----------------------

1. SMART MESSAGE WRITER
• Context-aware AI trained specifically on educational communications
• Understands the unique challenges and requirements of teacher-parent interaction
• Generates professional, warm, and appropriate messages for various scenarios
• Maintains consistent voice and tone across all communications
• Integrates seamlessly with existing school communication systems

2. TONE & STYLE ADJUSTMENT
• Multiple tone options: warm, professional, firm, neutral, empathetic
• Cultural sensitivity adjustments for international schools
• Age-appropriate language for different grade levels
• Subject-specific terminology and context
• Customizable templates for recurring communication needs

3. MULTILINGUAL SUPPORT
• Translation capabilities for 20+ languages
• Educational terminology preservation across languages
• Cultural context consideration in translations
• Support for ELL (English Language Learner) family communications
• Regional dialect and cultural norm awareness

4. REPORT COMMENT GENERATOR
• Curriculum-aligned comment suggestions
• Differentiated feedback options for diverse learners
• Progress tracking integration
• Standards-based language and terminology
• Export capabilities to major platforms (Google Classroom, Canvas, etc.)

5. PARENT COMMUNICATION TOOLS
• Email templates for common scenarios (behavior, academic, attendance)
• Phone call script generators for difficult conversations
• Meeting preparation and follow-up templates
• Crisis communication protocols
• Celebration and positive reinforcement messages

6. PRIVACY & SECURITY FEATURES
• No student data collection or storage
• GDPR compliant by design
• End-to-end encryption for all communications
• Audit trails for administrative oversight
• Role-based access controls for school implementations
