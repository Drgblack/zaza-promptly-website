Zaza Promptly Brand Guidelines

Brand Overview

Mission: Empower educators with AI tools that save time, improve communication, and enhance student learning.

Vision: Every teacher deserves time back. We build AI that understands the classroom.

Brand Personality: Professional, Empathetic, Teacher-focused, Trustworthy, Innovative

Logo Usage

Primary Logo

Zaza Promptly
  • Use the full "Zaza Promptly" wordmark with logo icon
  • Minimum size: 120px wide for digital, 1 inch for print
  • Clear space: Minimum 0.5x the logo height on all sides

Logo Variations

  • Full logo (primary)
  • Icon only (for small spaces)
  • White version (for dark backgrounds)
  • Monochrome version (single color applications)

Logo Don'ts

  • Don't stretch or distort the logo
  • Don't change colors without approval
  • Don't place on busy backgrounds without proper contrast
  • Don't separate the icon from the wordmark without permission

Color Palette

Primary Colors

Purple: #9333EA (Primary brand color)

Blue: #2563EB (Secondary brand color)

White: #FFFFFF (Background/text)

Secondary Colors

Slate: #64748B (Body text)

Green: #10B981 (Success/positive)

Pink: #EC4899 (Accent/highlight)

Usage Guidelines

  • Purple is the hero color - use prominently
  • Blue complements purple in gradients
  • Maintain proper contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum)

Typography

Primary Font: Inter

  • Headings: Inter Bold (700)
  • Body text: Inter Regular (400)
  • UI elements: Inter Medium (500)

Hierarchy

  • H1: 48-72px, Bold
  • H2: 36-48px, Bold
  • H3: 24-36px, Semibold
  • Body: 16-18px, Regular
  • Small text: 14px, Regular

Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

  • Supportive: We understand teacher challenges
  • Professional: Reliable and trustworthy
  • Conversational: Approachable, not corporate
  • Empowering: Focus on teacher success

Tone Guidelines

  • Use "you" and "your" to speak directly to teachers
  • Avoid jargon - speak in plain English
  • Be encouraging and positive
  • Acknowledge the hard work teachers do

Key Messages

  • "Save time, improve communication"
  • "Built by teachers, for teachers"
  • "AI that understands the classroom"
  • "Get your evenings back"

Photography & Imagery

Style Guidelines

  • Authentic classroom moments
  • Diverse educators and students
  • Natural lighting and expressions
  • Focus on technology integration

Image Treatment

  • Slight warm tone adjustment
  • Clean, uncluttered compositions
  • Teachers as heroes of the story
  • Technology as helpful tool, not dominant

Digital Guidelines

Website

  • Clean, modern design
  • Purple-to-blue gradients for CTAs
  • Plenty of white space
  • Mobile-first responsive design

Social Media

  • Consistent purple branding
  • Educational tips and teacher stories
  • Behind-the-scenes founder content
  • User-generated classroom successes
