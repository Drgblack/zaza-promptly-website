# After-Lunch Reset Routine

## Steps
1. **Greet at the door** with a smile and direct students to see the "Do Now" on the board—one quiet task, 3 minutes maximum.
2. **Use a calm countdown**: "When I reach 1, pencils down, eyes on me." Count slowly: 5-4-3-2-1.
3. **Lead box breathing** together: "4 counts in, hold for 4, out for 4, hold for 4." Model it yourself for 30-45 seconds.
4. **Give clear signal + script**: "Track me with your eyes. We're starting with [specific activity]."
5. **Praise publicly, redirect privately**: Log 3 positive behaviors you notice in the first 2 minutes.

## Scripts
- "Welcome back—find your seat and start the Do Now on the board. You've got 3 minutes."
- "When I say go: quiet breathing—4 in, hold, 4 out, hold. Ready? Go."
- "Thank you, Aisha and Ben, for being lesson-ready. Pencils down in 3...2...1."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Offer a fidget tool + front-row seat for students who struggle with self-regulation. Show breathing visually with hand gestures.
- **ELL**: Model each step with gestures. Write the routine steps on the board. Pair new ELL students with a buddy who can demonstrate.
