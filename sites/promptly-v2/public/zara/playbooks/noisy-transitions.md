# Noisy Transitions Management

## Steps
1. **Pre-teach the expectation**: "When you hear the chime, freeze like a statue—body still, voice off, eyes on me."
2. **Use a consistent signal**: Chime, clap pattern, or countdown. Same signal every time builds automatic response.
3. **Wait for 100% compliance** before speaking. Don't talk over noise—it teaches them they don't need to listen.
4. **Praise the positive first**: "I see table 3 is statue-still and ready. Table 1 is showing me respect with quiet bodies."
5. **Give one clear instruction**: "In 10 seconds, you'll walk—not run—to the carpet. Walking starts now."
6. **Practice during calm moments**: Run mini-transitions during lessons when energy is manageable.

## Scripts
- "Freeze like a statue in 3...2...1. Perfect! I can see everyone is ready to listen."
- "I'm looking for silent walking, hands at your sides, eyes forward. Show me your best."
- "Table 4 earned a point for statue-still listening. Who else can show me that focus?"

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Give a visual schedule card showing the steps. Allow fidget tools during listening time for movement-seeking students.
- **ELL**: Use visual cues (hand gestures) alongside verbal instructions. Create picture cards showing expected behavior during transitions.
