# Quick Formative Assessment Checks

## Steps
1. **Use thumbs up/down/sideways**: "Show me thumbs up if you're confident, thumbs sideways if you're getting there, thumbs down if you need help."
2. **Try fist-to-five**: Hold up fingers 1-5 to show understanding level. Anything under 3 means they need more support.
3. **Do exit ticket in 2 minutes**: One question on a sticky note before they leave. Review them to plan tomorrow's lesson.
4. **Use whiteboards**: Quick problems, one-word answers, or drawings. Everyone shows at the same time on your count.
5. **Ask "explain to your partner"**: Give 60 seconds to explain the concept to someone nearby. Listen in on conversations.
6. **Color-code understanding**: Green=got it, yellow=getting there, red=need help. Quick visual for you to see who needs support.

## Scripts
- "On the count of 3, show me your whiteboard answer. 1...2...3!"
- "Take 60 seconds to explain this concept to your partner using your own words. Go."
- "Before you line up, write one thing that made sense and one question you still have."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Allow drawing or symbols instead of only written responses. Give processing time before asking for responses.
- **ELL**: Pair with a supportive classmate for partner explanations. Accept responses in home language when appropriate.
