# Calm Corner & Self-Regulation Space

## Steps
1. **Design the space thoughtfully**: Soft seating, stress balls, breathing cards, timer, and a "how I'm feeling" chart. Keep it inviting, not punitive.
2. **Teach when calm, not in crisis**: During morning meeting or calm moments, practice using the calm corner tools and strategies.
3. **Make it a choice, not a sentence**: "You can choose to take a break in the calm corner, or choose to reset right here. What will help you?"
4. **Set clear expectations**: "Take the time you need to feel ready to learn. When you're ready, rejoin us quietly."
5. **Practice regulation strategies**: Deep breathing cards, counting to 10, name 5 things you can see, gentle stretches.
6. **Debrief after use**: "What helped you reset? How are you feeling now? What can I do to help you stay regulated?"

## Scripts
- "I can see you're having big feelings. The calm corner is ready when you need it."
- "Take the time you need to feel regulated and ready. We'll be here when you're ready to rejoin us."
- "I'm proud of you for recognizing you needed a break. That shows real self-awareness."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Create regulation tool cards with visual cues. Allow different amounts of time as needed—some students need 2 minutes, others need 10.
- **ELL**: Use emotion cards with pictures and simple words. Teach key phrases: "I need a break," "I'm ready," "I feel better."
