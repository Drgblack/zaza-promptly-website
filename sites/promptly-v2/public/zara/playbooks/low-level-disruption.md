# Low-Level Disruption (Non-Compliance)

## Steps
1. **Use proximity first**: Move closer to the student while continuing to teach. Often this resolves the issue without public attention.
2. **Give a private redirect**: Crouch down to their level and whisper, "I need you to [specific behavior]. Can you help me with that?"
3. **Offer a choice**: "You can choose to [expected behavior] here, or you can choose to finish this at the calm corner. What works for you?"
4. **Use the 3-before-me rule**: Before sending out, try proximity, private word, and choice. Document these attempts.
5. **Follow through calmly**: If they choose the consequence, guide them matter-of-factly. No arguing or negotiating.
6. **Rebuild after resolution**: "Thanks for getting back on track. How can I help you succeed next time?"

## Scripts
- "I need your eyes up here and your hands still. Can you show me that focus?"
- "You can choose to keep your materials on your desk, or choose to put them in the calm corner basket. What will help you learn?"
- "I see you made a good choice to refocus. That shows me you're ready to learn."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Offer movement breaks or fidget tools before behavior escalates. Use visual cues instead of only verbal redirects.
- **ELL**: Keep language simple and use gestures. Check for understanding: "Show me you know what I need to see."
