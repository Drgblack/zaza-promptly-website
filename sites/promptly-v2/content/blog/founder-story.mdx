## The Mission Takes Shape

With over 20 years of experience in education, corporate training, and digital transformation, I began to see how AI could be different. Not the cold, impersonal automation that threatens jobs, but warm, human-centered tools that give teachers back what they need most: time, energy, and purpose.

I taught myself to code in the early mornings and late nights. I learned about AI ethics, prompt engineering, and how to build products that teachers actually want to use. Every line of code was written between meetings, every design decision made with real teachers in mind.

## Why Zaza?

The name **Zaza** comes from my daughters, Viola and Solara. It represents joy, hope, and the future we're building for the next generation of learners and teachers.

Zaza Technologies isn't just another EdTech company. It's:
- **Independent**: Free from VC pressures that prioritize growth over impact
- **Teacher-first**: Built by someone who understands education from the inside out
- **Human-centered**: AI that amplifies human potential rather than replacing it
- **Family-driven**: Creating tools worthy of the educators I love
