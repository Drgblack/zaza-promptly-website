Yes! Promptly works seamlessly on all devices - phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

## Mobile-First Design

Promptly is designed for busy teachers who need to work on the go:

**Break time commenting:** Draft comments on your phone during a quick break
**Home flexibility:** Continue work on your laptop when you get home
**Tablet convenience:** Perfect for relaxed evening report writing

## Cross-Device Sync

Everything syncs automatically across your devices:
- Saved templates and preferences
- Work in progress  
- Comment history
- Personal style settings

Start a comment on your phone, finish it on your computer - it's that simple.

## Popular Mobile Workflows

**Quick parent emails:** Many teachers draft responses to parent queries on their phone during lunch breaks

**Report writing:** Add notes and observations throughout the day, then expand them into full comments later

**Template creation:** Save successful phrases and comments for reuse

## Device Requirements

**Phones:** Any modern smartphone with a web browser (iOS/Android)
**Tablets:** iPads, Android tablets, Surface devices  
**Computers:** Windows, Mac, Chromebooks, Linux
**Browsers:** Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge (latest versions recommended)

## No App Downloads Required

Promptly runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to install or update. Just bookmark the site and you're ready to go.

This also means your IT department doesn't need to approve app installations - it works on any device with internet access.

## Accessibility Features

- Large touch targets for mobile use
- Readable fonts on small screens
- Voice input support where available
- Screen reader compatibility
