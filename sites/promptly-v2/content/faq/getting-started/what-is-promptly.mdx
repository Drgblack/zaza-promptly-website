Zaza Promptly is an AI writing assistant designed specifically for UK teachers to help with report writing, parent communication, and student feedback.

Unlike generic AI tools, Promptly understands educational contexts, curriculum language, and the professional tone needed for school communications.

## Key Features

**Report comments:** Transform brief notes into professional, balanced feedback
**Parent emails:** Craft appropriate responses for any situation
**Snippet improvement:** Polish existing text to professional standards
**Multiple tones:** From warm and encouraging to direct and constructive

## How It's Different

- **Education-specific:** Built for teaching contexts, not general writing
- **GDPR compliant:** Safe for use with student information
- **No training on your data:** Your content stays private
- **Works on any device:** Mobile, tablet, or computer
