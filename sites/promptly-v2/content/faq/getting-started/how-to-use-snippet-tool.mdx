The Snippet Tool is our free way to try Promptly without signing up. It improves text you've already written.

## Simple Steps

1. Go to our [Snippet Tool](/#snippet)
2. Paste your existing text (report comment, email draft, etc.)
3. Tell Promptly how to improve it (e.g. "make more positive", "add specific examples")
4. Get an improved version instantly
5. Copy and use in your school system
