No. We never use your content to train AI models or improve our systems.

## Our Privacy Promise

**Zero training:** Your text input is never used for AI model training
**No storage:** Input is processed and immediately deleted
**No sharing:** Your content never leaves our secure processing environment
**No analysis:** We don't analyse or learn from your individual usage patterns

## How This Protects You

- Student information remains completely confidential
- Your writing style and content stay private
- No risk of your comments appearing in others' suggestions
- Full compliance with GDPR and UK data protection laws

## Third-Party AI Services

We use secure, privacy-compliant AI processing that:
- Processes your text without retaining it
- Never adds your content to training datasets  
- Operates under strict data processing agreements
- Meets educational privacy standards

This is fundamentally different from free AI tools that often use your input to improve their models.
