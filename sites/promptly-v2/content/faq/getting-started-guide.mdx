Getting started with Promptly is designed to be quick and simple - you can generate your first professional comment in under 2 minutes.

## Step 1: Try Before You Commit

**Free Snippet Tool:** Start with our [Snippet Tool](/#snippet) - no account required
- Paste in some text you've written
- Tell Promptly how to improve it
- See instant results

This gives you a feel for how Promptly works with your existing content.

## Step 2: Sign Up for Your Free Trial

1. Click "Start Free Trial" on any page
2. Enter your email address and create a password  
3. Choose your teaching context (subject, age group)
4. Start your 7-day free trial with full access

## Step 3: Generate Your First Comment

**Quick start method:**
1. Select a student scenario or write brief notes
2. Choose your communication style (professional, warm, direct)
3. Click generate and see your professional comment
4. Edit as needed and copy to your reporting system

## Step 4: Personalise Your Settings

**Tone preferences:** Set your default communication style
**Templates:** Save phrases and structures you use regularly
**Subject context:** Add details about your teaching area
**School specifics:** Include any terminology your school uses

## Common First-Time Questions

**"I don't know what to write in the prompt"**  
Just write bullet points about the student. Promptly turns brief notes into polished comments.

**"What if I don't like the result?"**  
Every suggestion is fully editable. Think of it as a first draft you can improve.

**"Will it work for my subject?"**  
Yes - from Early Years to A-Level, all subjects are supported.

## Your First Week Strategy

**Day 1-2:** Try the Snippet Tool with existing content
**Day 3-4:** Generate a few report comments  
**Day 5-6:** Test parent email communication
**Day 7:** Decide if the time savings are worth continuing

## Getting Comfortable

Most teachers find it helpful to:
- Start with low-stakes communications (positive parent emails)
- Use it alongside your normal process initially  
- Gradually trust it for more complex communications
- Build up your template library over time

## No Training Required

Unlike complex software, Promptly works in plain English. You don't need:
- Technical training courses
- Complex prompting skills
- IT department setup
- Special software installation
