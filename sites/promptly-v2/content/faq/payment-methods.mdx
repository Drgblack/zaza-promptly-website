We accept a variety of payment methods to suit both individual teachers and schools.

## Individual Teachers

**Credit/Debit Cards:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express
**Apple Pay:** Quick checkout on supported devices
**Google Pay:** Fast payment for Android users
**Bank Transfer:** Direct bank transfer for annual subscriptions

## Schools and Institutions

**Purchase Orders:** Full PO processing with proper invoicing
**Invoice Payment:** Net 30 payment terms for established institutions  
**Direct Debit:** Automated monthly or annual payments
**Bank Transfer:** Secure institutional transfers
**School Procurement Cards:** P-cards and institutional credit cards

## Billing Frequency

**Monthly:** Pay monthly with no long-term commitment
**Annual:** 20% discount when you pay for a full year upfront
**Quarterly:** Available for school licences on request

## Currency and Regions

**UK:** Payments in GBP (British Pounds)
**Europe:** EUR (Euro) payments available  
**International:** USD and other major currencies supported
**VAT handling:** Proper VAT calculation for UK/EU customers

## Security and Compliance

All payments are processed securely through:
- Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified)
- Bank-level encryption
- No card details stored on our servers
- Full payment audit trails

## Failed Payment Handling

**Automatic retry:** Failed payments are automatically retried
**Grace period:** 7-day grace period before service suspension
**Email notifications:** Advance warning of payment issues
**Easy update:** Simple process to update payment details

## Refunds and Disputes

**Pro-rata refunds:** If you cancel mid-billing cycle
**Dispute resolution:** Work with your bank/card provider
**Goodwill refunds:** Case-by-case consideration for special circumstances

## School Finance Teams

We understand school procurement processes:

**Official invoices:** Proper tax invoices with all required details
**Budget codes:** Can include your budget/department codes
**Monthly statements:** Detailed usage reports for finance teams
**Multi-year agreements:** Longer-term contracts available

Questions about payment options? [Contact our billing team](/contact) - we're experienced in working with both individual teachers and school finance departments.
