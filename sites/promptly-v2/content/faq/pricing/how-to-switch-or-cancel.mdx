You can change your subscription or cancel anytime with just a few clicks - no phone calls required.

## Switching Plans

**Upgrade:** Takes effect immediately, you're only charged the difference
**Downgrade:** Takes effect at your next billing cycle
**Billing frequency:** Switch between monthly and annual anytime

## Cancelling Your Subscription

1. Log into your account
2. Go to billing settings
3. Click "Cancel subscription"
4. Confirm your choice
5. Done!

## What Happens After Cancelling

- **No future charges** - billing stops immediately
- **Keep access** until your current billing period ends
- **Data retention** - templates saved for 90 days in case you return
- **Reactivation** - restart anytime with one click

## Downgrading to Free

Instead of cancelling, you can switch to our free tier (5 comments per month) - perfect for occasional use.

## No Penalties

- No cancellation fees
- No early termination charges
- No requirement to speak to anyone
- Immediate cancellation confirmation

Before cancelling, try our [support team](/contact) - often there's a simple solution to whatever issue you're experiencing.
