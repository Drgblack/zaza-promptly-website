Promptly currently supports English, with additional languages planned based on teacher demand.

## Current Language Support

**Full support:** English (UK, US, Australian, Canadian variants)
**Educational contexts:** British curriculum terminology, UK school systems
**Regional adaptation:** Automatically adjusts for local spelling and terminology

## Planned Languages

Based on teacher requests, we're developing support for:
- **Spanish** (for international schools)
- **French** (for bilingual programmes)
- **Welsh** (for Welsh-medium education)
- **German** (for international contexts)

## Multilingual Teaching Support

For teachers working with multilingual families:
- Generate comments in English that are clear and accessible
- Simple language options for non-native English-speaking parents
- Cultural sensitivity in communication tone and approach

## Translation Features

While we don't currently offer translation services, Promptly can help you:
- Write clearer English that translates better
- Use simpler vocabulary for international families
- Structure communications for better understanding

## International Schools

Promptly works well for:
- British international schools worldwide
- IB programme schools using English
- American curriculum schools
- Schools transitioning to English instruction

## Language Development Timeline

**2025:** Enhanced UK English features and Welsh support pilot
**2026:** Spanish and French for international schools
**Future:** Additional languages based on user feedback
