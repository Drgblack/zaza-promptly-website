**Level 1:** Initial support via email or help centre

## Getting Better Help

To get the fastest resolution:
- Include specific error messages if any
- Tell us your browser and device type
- Describe what you were trying to do
- Mention if you're on school or personal networks

## Emergency Support

For school licences experiencing widespread issues:
- Priority escalation to senior technical team
- Direct phone support when needed
- Temporary workarounds while permanent fixes are developed
