Promptly has multiple built-in safety systems specifically designed to prevent false or inappropriate content about students.

## Safety Layers

**Content filtering:** Prevents inappropriate language or suggestions
**Context awareness:** Ensures age-appropriate communication for different year groups
**Tone validation:** Maintains professional teaching standards
**Hallucination prevention:** Won't invent facts about students or their performance

## How We Prevent False Information

Unlike general AI tools, Promptly is designed specifically for education:

**No student data storage:** We don't keep information about individual students
**Template-based generation:** Works from your input, not imagined details
**Professional constraints:** Built-in limits ensure educationally appropriate content
**Teacher oversight:** Every suggestion requires your professional review and approval

## What This Means in Practice

**Promptly will:** Help you express your observations more clearly
**Promptly won't:** Invent details about student behaviour or academic performance

**Promptly will:** Suggest professional language for difficult conversations  
**Promptly won't:** Create inappropriate content or unprofessional suggestions

**Promptly will:** Help structure your thoughts into clear, balanced comments
**Promptly won't:** Make assessment judgements about students it's never seen

## Your Professional Judgement Remains Key

Promptly is designed to support your professional expertise, not replace it:

- You provide the observations and insights
- You make all decisions about what's appropriate
- You maintain complete control over final content
- You're always the professional authority on your students

## Quality Assurance

Every output is checked against educational communication standards before it reaches you. This includes:

- Professional language requirements
- Age-appropriate tone and content
- Balanced, constructive feedback principles  
- Safeguarding-aware communication

## Compare to Other AI Tools

**ChatGPT/General AI:** Can invent plausible-sounding but false details about students
**Promptly:** Only works with information you provide, adds no fictional elements
