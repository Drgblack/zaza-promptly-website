Absolutely. Promptly works across all subjects and age groups from Reception to Year 13.

## Subject Coverage

**Core subjects:** Maths, English, Science with curriculum-aligned language
**Specialist subjects:** Art, Music, PE, Design & Technology, Languages  
**SEND and support:** Adapted communication for additional needs
**Cross-curricular:** Projects and skills that span multiple subjects

## Age-Appropriate Communication

**Early Years (Reception/Year 1):** Simple, positive language focused on development and play-based learning

**Primary (Years 2-6):** Clear communication about skills development, behaviour, and academic progress

**Secondary (Years 7-13):** More sophisticated language addressing subject-specific skills, GCSE/A-Level preparation, and independent learning

## Real Examples

**Reception:** "Oliver shows great enthusiasm for counting activities and enjoys exploring numbers through play. He confidently counts to 10 and is beginning to recognise numerals in different contexts."

**Year 8 Science:** "Emma demonstrates excellent practical skills in the laboratory and shows a strong understanding of experimental method. Her written explanations would benefit from more detailed scientific vocabulary."

**Year 12 English:** "James writes with sophistication and insight, showing particular strength in analysis of literary techniques. His essay structure continues to develop, with increasing confidence in presenting complex arguments."

## Regional Variations

Promptly adapts to different educational systems:
- English National Curriculum terminology
- Scottish Curriculum for Excellence  
- Welsh Curriculum language
- International schools and IB programmes

Primary teachers especially love the parent communication features, whilst secondary teachers find the report writing tools invaluable during assessment periods.
