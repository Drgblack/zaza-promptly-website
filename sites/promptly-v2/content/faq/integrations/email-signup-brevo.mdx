We use Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) for secure, GDPR-compliant email management and teacher resources.

## What We Send

**Welcome emails:** Getting started guides and tips
**Educational resources:** Free templates, guides, and teaching tips
**Product updates:** New features and improvements  
**Optional newsletter:** Weekly teaching productivity tips

## Your Email Preferences

**Full control:** Unsubscribe from any email type anytime
**Preference centre:** Choose which emails you want to receive
**One-click unsubscribe:** Stop all emails instantly if preferred
**No selling:** We never sell or share your email address

## GDPR Compliance

- **Explicit consent:** You choose what emails to receive
- **Easy unsubscribe:** One-click removal from all lists
- **Data protection:** EU-based email processing with Brevo
- **Transparency:** Clear information about what emails we send

## Educational Content

We send genuinely useful content for teachers:
- Free AI prompt templates
- Time-saving teaching tips
- Research-backed productivity strategies
- Early access to new features

## Managing Your Preferences

Update your email preferences anytime:
- Click "manage preferences" in any email
- Log into your account settings
- [Contact us](/contact) to update manually

No spam, no selling your data - just helpful content for busy teachers.
