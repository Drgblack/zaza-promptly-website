You can absolutely write your own comments first and use Promptly to polish them up. This is one of the most popular ways teachers use our tool.

## Using the Snippet Tool

1. Visit our free [Snippet Tool](/#snippet) 
2. Write your draft comment in plain English
3. Tell Promptly what you'd like improved (e.g. "make this more positive" or "add specific examples")
4. Get an improved version that maintains your voice

## What Promptly Can Improve

**Tone and clarity:** Make comments more positive, professional, or constructive
**Structure:** Organise your thoughts into clear, logical paragraphs  
**Language:** Polish grammar and spelling whilst keeping your natural voice
**Balance:** Help achieve the right mix of praise and areas for development
**Specificity:** Add concrete examples whilst maintaining appropriate generality

## Example Workflow

**Your draft:** "Sarah is doing okay in maths but needs to try harder with her times tables"

**Promptly's improvement:** "Sarah demonstrates solid understanding of mathematical concepts and approaches problems with confidence. To further strengthen her numeracy skills, focused practice with times tables would help her achieve even greater fluency and speed in calculations."

The key is that you're still the professional making all the decisions - Promptly just helps you express your thoughts more clearly and professionally.
