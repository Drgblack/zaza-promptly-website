## Maintaining Your Professional Voice

Promptly understands that every teacher has their own communication style:

**Warm and encouraging:** "Sophie has shown wonderful progress..."
**Direct and practical:** "Tom needs to focus on improving his..."  
**Balanced and constructive:** "Emma demonstrates strength in... and would benefit from..."

## Custom Templates

You can save your own comment templates and phrases, so Promptly learns exactly how you like to express common ideas.

The goal isn't to replace your voice - it's to help you express your professional thoughts more clearly and efficiently.
