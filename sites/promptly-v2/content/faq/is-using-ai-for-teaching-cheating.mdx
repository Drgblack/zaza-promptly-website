No, absolutely not. Using AI tools like Zaza Promptly for administrative tasks is working smarter, not cheating.

## What Promptly Actually Does

Promptly helps with the time-consuming bits:
- Drafting parent emails and communications
- Writing report comments and feedback
- Improving existing text you've written

You maintain complete control over:
- What gets sent to parents
- Your teaching methods and pedagogy  
- All educational decisions about your students
- Your professional voice and tone

Teachers using Promptly report saving 3-5 hours per week on administrative tasks. That's time you can spend on:
- Actual teaching and lesson planning
- Building relationships with students
- Professional development
- Work-life balance
