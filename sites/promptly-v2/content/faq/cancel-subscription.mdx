Yes, you can cancel your Promptly subscription at any time with just one click - no penalties, no phone calls required.

## Simple Cancellation Process

1. Log into your account
2. Go to billing settings  
3. Click "Cancel subscription"
4. Confirm your choice
5. Done!

## What Happens When You Cancel

**Immediate effect:** No future billing charges
**Continued access:** You keep full access until your current billing period ends
**Data retention:** Your templates and preferences are saved for 90 days
**Reactivation:** Easy to restart your subscription if you change your mind

## No Hidden Fees

- No cancellation penalties
- No early termination fees  
- No "processing charges"
- No requirement to speak to anyone

## Grace Period

If you cancel by mistake or change your mind, you can reactivate your subscription instantly within your billing period.

After 90 days, we'll delete your account data (as required by GDPR), but you can always sign up again with a fresh account.

## Why We Make It Easy

We're confident that Promptly saves teachers time and reduces stress. If it's not working for you, we don't want to make leaving difficult.

Most teachers who try cancelling end up reactivating within a few days once they realise how much extra work they're doing without Promptly!

## Before You Cancel

If you're having issues with Promptly, please [contact our support team](/contact) first. Often there's a simple solution or feature you might not know about.

Common reasons people consider cancelling (and solutions):
- **"It's too expensive"** → Try our free tier or ask about school discounts
- **"I don't use it enough"** → Our free tier might be perfect for occasional use
- **"It doesn't sound like me"** → We can help customise the tone and style
