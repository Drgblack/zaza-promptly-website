Yes, we provide comprehensive data protection documentation required by UK schools.

## Available Documentation

**Data Processing Agreement (DPA):** Standard agreement covering our data processing activities under GDPR
**Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA):** Risk assessment documentation for school use
**Privacy Impact Assessment:** Technical security and privacy evaluation
**Compliance certificates:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, and other relevant certifications

## What's Covered in Our DPA

- Lawful basis for processing personal data
- Categories of data subjects and personal data
- Processing purposes and retention periods
- Technical and organisational security measures
- Sub-processor agreements and data transfer safeguards
- Data subject rights and breach notification procedures

## Custom Documentation

For schools with specific requirements:
- Tailored DPA clauses for your policies
- Additional security documentation  
- Technical integration specifications
- Audit trail and reporting procedures

## Working with Your DPO

We regularly work with school Data Protection Officers and can provide:
- Technical answers to compliance questions
- Documentation reviews and revisions
- Ongoing compliance monitoring support
