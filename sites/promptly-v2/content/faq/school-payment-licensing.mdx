## Pricing Structure

School licences are seat-based, so you only pay for the teachers who actually use Promptly:

- **5-15 teachers:** £6 per teacher per month
- **16-30 teachers:** £5 per teacher per month  
- **31+ teachers:** £4 per teacher per month
- **MAT/District pricing:** Custom pricing for multiple schools

Annual billing includes a 20% discount.
