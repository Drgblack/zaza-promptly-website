Promptly adapts to different communication styles and automatically adjusts language complexity for various audiences.

## Available Tones

**Professional:** Standard school communication tone
**Warm & encouraging:** Positive, supportive language
**Direct & constructive:** Clear, straightforward feedback
**Empathetic:** Sensitive approach for difficult topics
**Celebratory:** Highlighting achievements and progress

## Reading Levels

**Parent communication:** Clear, accessible language for all families
**Professional reports:** Appropriate complexity for official documents
**Student feedback:** Age-appropriate language from Reception to Year 13
**Colleague communication:** Professional terminology when needed

## Automatic Adjustments

Promptly considers:
- **Student age group** (Early Years vs A-Level language)
- **Communication purpose** (celebration vs concern)
- **Audience type** (parents vs colleagues vs students)
- **Cultural sensitivity** for diverse school communities

## Customisation

You can request specific adjustments: "make this warmer", "use simpler language", "more formal tone", or "add more detail".

The AI learns your preferences over time and can match your natural communication style.
