Promptly helps you add concrete details whilst maintaining appropriate professional boundaries and student privacy.

## Balancing Specific and General

**Too vague:** "Needs to improve writing"
**Better:** "Would benefit from focusing on paragraph structure and using varied sentence lengths"

**Too personal:** "Struggles because of issues at home"
**Professional:** "Would benefit from additional support to maintain focus during independent tasks"

## Adding Concrete Details

Promptly suggests specific examples that are:
- **Observable behaviours** rather than assumptions
- **Academic skills** rather than personal circumstances
- **Actionable next steps** rather than vague improvements
- **Curriculum-linked** progress indicators

## Professional Language Patterns

**Instead of:** "Bad at..."
**Use:** "Developing skills in..." or "Would benefit from practice with..."

**Instead of:** "Always/never..."
**Use:** "Often demonstrates..." or "Sometimes struggles with..."

## Maintaining Privacy

Promptly helps you reference student progress without revealing:
- Personal family information
- Sensitive circumstances
- Individual comparisons with other students
- Specific incidents or behaviours
