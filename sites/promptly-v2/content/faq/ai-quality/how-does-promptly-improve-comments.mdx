Promptly enhances your comments by improving structure, tone, and professional language whilst maintaining your insights and observations.

## Key Improvements

**Professional language:** "Good at maths" becomes "Demonstrates solid mathematical understanding"
**Balanced structure:** Combines strengths with next steps naturally
**Specific examples:** Adds concrete details without being too personal
**Consistent tone:** Maintains appropriate formality across all comments

## What Promptly Does

- **Expands brief notes** into full, professional comments
- **Improves sentence flow** and paragraph structure  
- **Balances praise and development** areas appropriately
- **Matches your school's** communication style
- **Ensures age-appropriate** language for different year groups

## What Stays Yours

Your professional judgement, observations about students, and decisions about what to include remain completely under your control.

Promptly simply helps you express your thoughts more clearly and professionally.
