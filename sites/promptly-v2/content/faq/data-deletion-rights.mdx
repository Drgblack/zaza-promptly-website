Yes, you can delete your data and account completely at any time. This is your right under GDPR and UK data protection law.

## Your Data Rights

**Right to erasure:** Request complete deletion of your personal data
**Right to rectification:** Correct any inaccurate information we hold
**Right to portability:** Download your data in a machine-readable format  
**Right to access:** See exactly what data we have about you

## What Gets Deleted

When you request account deletion:

**Personal information:** Email, name, payment details
**Usage data:** Login history, feature usage statistics
**Content:** Saved templates, comment history, preferences
**Account data:** Settings, customisations, subscription details

## Deletion Process

**Self-service deletion:**
1. Log into your account
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Click "Delete Account"  
4. Confirm your choice
5. Everything is permanently removed within 48 hours

**Email request:**
Send a deletion request to support@zazatechnologies.com from your registered email address. We'll process it within 72 hours as required by law.

## What Happens After Deletion

**Immediate:** Account becomes inaccessible  
**48 hours:** All personal data permanently removed from active systems
**30 days:** Data removed from backup systems
**90 days:** Complete erasure from all systems including logs

## Data We Cannot Delete

Under UK law, we must retain some information for:

**Financial records:** Payment history for tax purposes (6 years)
**Legal compliance:** Fraud prevention data (as required by law)
**Anonymised analytics:** Non-personal usage statistics (cannot be linked back to you)

## Before You Delete

**Download your data:** We can provide a copy of your templates and settings
**Consider free tier:** You might want to keep a free account instead of full deletion
**Reactivation:** Once deleted, you'd need to create a new account to use Promptly again

## School Account Considerations

For school licences:
- Individual teachers can request their personal data deletion
- School administrators control institutional data
- We work with schools to balance individual rights with institutional needs

## Proving Your Identity

For security, we may ask you to verify your identity before processing deletion requests, especially for email requests.

## Legal Basis

This deletion right is provided under:
- UK Data Protection Act 2018
- EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- Our commitment to user privacy
