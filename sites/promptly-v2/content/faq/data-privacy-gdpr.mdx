Yes, Zaza Promptly is fully GDPR compliant and designed specifically for the strict privacy requirements of UK schools.

## Privacy Protection Features

**No data retention:** We don't store your student information after processing
**EU-only servers:** All data processing happens within the European Union
**Bank-level encryption:** Your data is protected with industry-standard security
**Zero training:** Your content never trains other AI models or gets shared

## GDPR Compliance

We provide:
- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) for schools
- Full audit trails for compliance officers  
- Right to erasure (data deletion) on request
- Transparent privacy policies in plain English

## What Data We Process

**We process:** The text you input to generate comments (temporarily, during your session)
**We don't store:** Student names, sensitive information, or personal data
**We don't access:** Your school's systems, databases, or other applications

## For IT Teams and Data Officers

We're happy to work with your IT team or Data Protection Officer to ensure compliance with your specific policies.

Common documentation we provide:
- Technical security specifications
- Data flow diagrams  
- Compliance certificates
- Custom DPA agreements

## International Standards

Beyond GDPR, we also comply with:
- UK Data Protection Act 2018
- ISO 27001 security standards
- SOC 2 Type II certification
