Promptly is specifically designed for teachers, whilst ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI that wasn't built for education.

## Key Differences

### Education-Specific Design
Promptly understands teaching contexts, curriculum requirements, and appropriate communication styles for different age groups. It's been trained specifically on educational best practices.

ChatGPT is a general tool that might suggest inappropriate content or use the wrong tone for school communications.

### Safety and Accuracy  
Promptly has built-in safeguards against "hallucination" - it won't invent false information about students or suggest inappropriate content.

ChatGPT can generate plausible-sounding but completely false information, which is dangerous when writing about real students.

### Privacy and Compliance
- **Promptly:** GDPR-compliant, UK/EU data processing, no data retention
- **ChatGPT:** Stores conversations, trains on your data, US-based servers

### Professional Voice
Promptly maintains professional teaching standards and can match your personal communication style.

ChatGPT might sound robotic, overly casual, or use inappropriate language for school contexts.

### Ease of Use
Promptly is designed for busy teachers - no complex prompting required. Just describe what you need and it works.

ChatGPT requires you to learn how to write effective prompts and often gives irrelevant responses.

## Created by Educators, For Educators
