# Saving 4+ Hours Per Week with AI-Powered Comments

As Head of Year 7 at Greenfield Academy, I was drowning in paperwork. Between managing 180 students and writing meaningful report comments, I was working 60+ hour weeks just to keep up.

## The Challenge

Every term, I needed to write detailed comments for each of my students across multiple subjects. The process was exhausting:

- **3-4 hours per evening** writing individual comments
- **Repetitive phrasing** that felt impersonal
- **Inconsistent tone** across different subjects
- **Weekend work** just to meet deadlines

I was burning out, and my family time was suffering.

## Discovering Promptly

A colleague recommended Promptly during a particularly stressful reporting period. I was skeptical at first - could AI really understand the nuances of student progress?

The first thing that impressed me was how Promptly didn't try to replace my judgment. Instead, it amplified my insights by helping me articulate them more effectively.

## The Transformation

Within my first week using Promptly:

### Time Savings
- Comments that took 10-15 minutes now take 2-3 minutes
- **Reduced my evening workload by 75%**
- Freed up 4+ hours weekly for lesson planning and family time

### Quality Improvements
- More consistent, professional tone across all comments
- Better use of educational terminology
- Parents specifically mentioned the clarity of feedback

### Reduced Stress
- No more Sunday evening panic sessions
- Confident that every comment meets our school's standards
- Energy restored for actual teaching

## Real Examples

**Before Promptly:**
"Jake has had a good term in English. He completes his work and tries hard. Sometimes he needs reminding to focus. His writing is improving."

**With Promptly:**
"Jake has demonstrated consistent effort in English this term, showing particular growth in his creative writing. While he occasionally requires gentle redirection to maintain focus during independent tasks, his willingness to incorporate feedback has led to noticeable improvements in his narrative structure and vocabulary choices."

## The Ripple Effect

The time I saved didn't just benefit me - it transformed my entire approach to student support:

- **More one-on-one time** with students who need extra support
- **Enhanced lesson planning** with the energy I'd previously spent on comments
- **Better work-life balance** that made me a more effective teacher and parent

## Advice for Other Educators

If you're on the fence about AI tools, I understand the hesitation. But Promptly isn't about replacing your expertise - it's about amplifying it.

Start small with just one class or subject. You'll quickly see how it can transform not just your reporting, but your entire relationship with administrative tasks.

## Looking Forward

Six months later, I can't imagine going back to manual comment writing. Promptly has given me back the time and energy to focus on what I love most about teaching - actually working with students.

The tool has evolved too, with new features that make differentiation and progress tracking even more seamless. I'm excited to see how it continues to support educators like me who want to do their best work without sacrificing their wellbeing.
