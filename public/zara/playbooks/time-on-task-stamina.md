# Time-on-Task Stamina Building

## Steps
1. **Start with achievable chunks**: Begin with 8-10 minutes of focused work, then gradually increase by 2-3 minutes each week.
2. **Use a visible timer**: Let students see how much time remains. "We're going for 10 minutes of silent, focused work."
3. **Build in brain breaks**: After sustained work, do 60 seconds of movement, stretching, or breathing before continuing.
4. **Track progress visually**: Use a class chart showing how long students can focus. Celebrate improvements together.
5. **Teach self-monitoring**: Give students a simple checklist: Am I on task? Am I focused? What do I need to refocus?
6. **Provide choice within structure**: "You can work at your desk, the table, or the floor—your job is 15 minutes of quality focus."

## Scripts
- "We're building our focus muscles today. Let's see if we can beat yesterday's 12-minute record."
- "If you notice your brain wandering, that's normal. Just bring it back to your work—that's how focus grows stronger."
- "I saw amazing stamina from this class today. You focused for 14 minutes straight!"

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Break work into smaller chunks with movement breaks. Use fidget tools or standing desks to support attention needs.
- **ELL**: Provide visual task reminders and check in quietly to ensure they understand what focused work looks like.
