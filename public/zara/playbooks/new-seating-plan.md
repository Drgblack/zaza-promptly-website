# Starting a New Seating Plan

## Steps
1. **Introduce with purpose**: "I'm trying a new seating plan to help everyone learn better. This is a 2-week trial."
2. **Set clear expectations**: "Your job is to show me you can focus, help your new neighbors, and follow our classroom rules."
3. **Give a settling activity** immediately: Silent reading, journal prompt, or puzzle for the first 10 minutes in new seats.
4. **Monitor proximity patterns**: Walk the room more in week 1. Note which partnerships work and which need tweaking.
5. **Build in reflection time**: "Rate your focus today 1-5. What helped you learn in your new spot?"
6. **Adjust strategically**: Make 2-3 small changes after week 1 based on observations, not complaints.

## Scripts
- "Find your name card and sit down quietly. Once you're seated, start the journal prompt on your desk."
- "This seating plan helps me make sure everyone can see, hear, and participate. Let's try it for 2 weeks."
- "I'm watching for good citizenship—helping neighbors, staying focused, contributing to learning."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Place students with attention needs near your teaching area but not isolated. Pair supportive peers nearby.
- **ELL**: Seat new English learners near patient, helpful classmates who can model without doing the work for them.
