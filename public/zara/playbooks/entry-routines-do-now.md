# Entry Routines & Do Now Systems

## Steps
1. **Post the Do Now before students arrive**: Same spot every day (board, desk, or screen). Make it visible from the doorway.
2. **Greet at the door with purpose**: "Good morning! Your Do Now is on the board—5 minutes of silent work to get your brain ready."
3. **Keep Do Now short and doable**: Review yesterday's concept, preview today's lesson, or brain warm-up. 3-5 minutes maximum.
4. **Use a consistent routine**: Enter quietly, unpack materials, start Do Now, wait for signal. Same sequence builds automaticity.
5. **Set the timer visibly**: Students can see how much time remains. When timer goes off, pencils down, eyes forward.
6. **Connect to the lesson**: "Your Do Now reminded us of fractions. Today we're extending that thinking to..."

## Scripts
- "Welcome! Your brain warm-up is ready on the board. You have 5 minutes of silent thinking time."
- "I can see everyone is focused and ready. This is exactly what successful learners look like."
- "Your Do Now showed me you remember yesterday's lesson. Let's build on that learning today."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Provide choice in Do Now response format—writing, drawing, or verbal response to a partner. Offer movement jobs for kinesthetic learners.
- **ELL**: Include visual supports in Do Now tasks. Use sentence frames or word banks when helpful.
