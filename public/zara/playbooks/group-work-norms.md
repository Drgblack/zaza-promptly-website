# Group Work Norms & Management

## Steps
1. **Establish clear roles**: Timekeeper, materials manager, recorder, presenter. Rotate roles each activity so everyone practices leadership.
2. **Teach voice levels explicitly**: Level 0 (silent), Level 1 (whisper), Level 2 (table talk), Level 3 (presentation voice). Post visual reminders.
3. **Set participation expectations**: "Everyone talks, everyone listens, everyone contributes ideas." Use talking chips or timer for equal participation.
4. **Create accountability systems**: Each person signs their section. Groups peer-evaluate using a simple rubric you provide.
5. **Monitor with proximity**: Move constantly during group work. Crouch down to join conversations, not hover above.
6. **Build in report-backs**: Quick 1-minute shares from each group keep everyone accountable and engaged.

## Scripts
- "You have 15 minutes for Level 2 table talk. I want to hear productive conversation and see everyone participating."
- "Before you start, decide who's your timekeeper and who's managing materials. You have 30 seconds to organize."
- "I'm looking for groups where everyone is leaning in, contributing ideas, and helping the group succeed."

## Differentiation
- **SEND**: Assign specific roles that match student strengths. Provide written role cards with clear expectations and sentence starters.
- **ELL**: Pair with supportive peers. Provide key vocabulary and sentence frames for group discussions.
