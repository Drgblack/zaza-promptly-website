# Usage Limits & Fair Use

**Free Usage**: 3 conversations per day with Zara assistant on this website. Resets every 24 hours.

**Promptly App Limits**: 
- Starter Plan: 500 AI generations per month
- Pro Plan: Unlimited AI generations and priority processing

**Message Length**: Maximum 2,000 characters per message to ensure quality responses.

**Rate Limiting**: Maximum 1 message per minute to prevent abuse and ensure server stability.

**Fair Use Policy**: No automated/bot usage. Personal educational use only. Commercial use requires Pro subscription.

**Content Restrictions**: No inappropriate, harmful, or non-educational content. System automatically flags policy violations.

**Geographic Limits**: Available worldwide except in restricted countries due to data protection regulations.

**Language Support**: Full support for English, German, French, Spanish, Italian. Limited support for other languages.

**Concurrent Users**: No limit on simultaneous users, but response time may vary during peak hours.

**Data Export**: Export your generated content anytime. No vendor lock-in.
