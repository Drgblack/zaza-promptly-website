# Privacy & Data Protection

**Zero Student Data Storage**: We never store student names, grades, or personal information. All student data is processed in memory and immediately discarded.

**FERPA & GDPR Compliant**: Full compliance with education privacy laws in US and EU. Regular audits ensure continued compliance.

**Data Processing Location**: All data processed within EU servers (Frankfurt, Germany) with end-to-end encryption.

**What We Store**: Only anonymous usage analytics and your account preferences. No message content is retained after processing.

**Teacher Data**: Your account email and subscription status only. No lesson content or student information is ever saved.

**Third-Party Sharing**: Zero. We never share, sell, or provide teacher or student data to advertisers or other companies.

**Data Deletion**: You can request complete account deletion at any time. All data removed within 30 days.

**Security Measures**: 256-bit encryption, regular security audits, SOC 2 compliance, and isolated processing environments.

For detailed privacy policy: https://zazapromptly.com/privacy
