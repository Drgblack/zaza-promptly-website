## Content Optimization Guidelines

### On-Page SEO Requirements
- **H1:** Include primary target keyword naturally
- **H2s:** Include 2-3 secondary keywords across subheadings  
- **Meta Description:** 150-160 characters with primary keyword and CTA
- **URL:** /blog/primary-keyword-phrase (hyphenated)
- **Images:** Descriptive alt text with keywords, WebP format
- **Internal Links:** Minimum 5-7 strategic internal links per post
- **External Links:** 2-3 authoritative sources (education research, government data)

### Content Structure Template
1. **Introduction (200-300 words)**
   - Hook with teacher pain point
   - Primary keyword in first 100 words
   - Clear value proposition

2. **Main Content Sections (1,500-2,500 words)**
   - H2 subheadings with secondary keywords
   - Bullet points and numbered lists
   - Real teacher examples/case studies
   - Screenshots/visuals where relevant

3. **FAQ Section (300-500 words)**
   - Target voice search queries
   - Schema markup for featured snippets
   - 3-5 common questions with detailed answers

4. **Conclusion with CTA (150-200 words)**
   - Recap key benefits
   - Strong call-to-action to product/trial
   - Related content suggestions

### AI SEO Optimization
- **Answer Engine Optimization:** Include direct answers to common questions in first paragraph
- **Semantic HTML:** Use `<dl>`, `<dt>`, `<dd>` for definitions and Q&A
- **Featured Snippet Optimization:** Numbered lists, comparison tables, step-by-step guides
- **Voice Search:** Include conversational "how to" and "what is" phrases

### Distribution Strategy
1. **Primary:** Blog publication with full SEO optimization
2. **Social Media:** LinkedIn articles targeting education professionals  
3. **Email Newsletter:** Weekly digest to teacher subscriber base
4. **Guest Content:** Education technology publications and teacher forums
5. **Repurposing:** Convert to webinar content, infographics, podcast episodes
