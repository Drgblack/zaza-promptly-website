# Zaza Coach – Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Zaza Coach?
Zaza Coach is your AI-powered co-pilot for feedback, coaching, and onboarding. It helps managers and HR professionals deliver timely, personalised support at scale—without burning out.

## Who is it for?
- Managers who lead people but aren't trained in coaching.
- HR professionals designing onboarding and growth systems.
- Executive and leadership coaches managing multiple clients.
- Remote/hybrid teams who need asynchronous support.

## How does it work?
You choose the intent (e.g., onboarding, review, nudge), give some context (e.g., role, tone), and Zaza generates draft messages, plans, or prompts. You stay in control, Zaza speeds up the heavy lifting.

## What languages are supported?
Zaza Coach supports English with roadmap support for Spanish, German, and French in Q4 2025.

## Can I customise the tone?
Yes. You can select from built-in tones (friendly, formal, assertive, warm) or train a custom tone profile based on your past communication.

## What about privacy?
All coaching and HR data is encrypted. No training data is used from private company interactions. Zaza Coach is GDPR-compliant and ISO-27001 ready.

## Can it be used for feedback AND onboarding?
Yes. Zaza Coach includes:
- Feedback generators (with tone and intent controls).
- Onboarding message templates.
- Check-in schedules and nudges.
