# Zaza Coach – Integrations

Zaza Coach integrates seamlessly with tools professionals already use to manage communication, feedback, and HR workflows. These integrations make it easy to embed AI-powered coaching directly into daily operations.

## 🔄 Core Integrations

### Slack
- Trigger feedback requests directly from conversations.
- Deliver coaching tips and nudges via channels or DMs.
- Use slash commands to pull in coaching templates.

### Microsoft Teams
- Use cards to collect feedback and surface insights during 1:1s.
- Schedule and receive reminders for coaching check-ins.
- Sync notes and nudges to shared teams or private chats.

### Google Workspace
- Draft coaching emails and meeting follow-ups with Gmail integration.
- Trigger AI guidance from Calendar-based 1:1s and reviews.
- Sync feedback summaries to shared Docs.

### CSV & Manual Uploads
- Upload lists of team members or coaching clients.
- Trigger onboarding flows or development plans in batch.

## 🚀 Upcoming Integrations

- **Workday**: Lifecycle milestone triggers (e.g., promotion, exit).
- **BambooHR**: Sync goals, notes, and review cycles.

## 🧠 API & Webhooks

Zaza Coach will provide API access and webhook support for:
- Triggering custom events (e.g., new hire, completed milestone).
- Fetching coaching insights from external dashboards.
- Syncing with internal LMS or HRIS systems.
