
# 📘 Zaza Promptly – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Zaza Promptly is designed to simplify and elevate the process of writing student feedback. Below you'll find the most common questions we receive, along with helpful, detailed answers.

---

## 🟦 GENERAL USAGE

**Q: Is Zaza Promptly free to use?**  
A: Yes, you can use Zaza Promptly for free with a monthly limit of 5 AI-generated comment sessions. This allows you to explore the tool and experience its value before committing to a paid plan.

**Q: How does Zaza Promptly differ from ChatGPT?**  
A: While it uses GPT-4 under the hood, Zaza Promptly is *not* a general-purpose chatbot. It’s fine-tuned, contextually constrained, and embedded with education-specific guardrails to reduce hallucination, keep tone appropriate, and ensure alignment with curriculum standards.

**Q: What happens after the free limit is reached?**  
A: You'll still have access to your account and saved snippets, but new comment generations will be paused until your monthly limit resets or you upgrade to a Pro or School Licence.

**Q: Is there a trial for Pro?**  
A: Yes, new users are eligible for a 7-day trial of the Pro plan upon signup, giving you unlimited access to all core features during the period.

---

## 💳 BILLING & LICENSING

**Q: What payment options are available?**  
A: You can pay monthly or annually (20% discount for annual). Accepted methods include credit/debit cards. School Licences support purchase orders and invoicing.

**Q: Can schools or districts pay by invoice?**  
A: Absolutely. We support invoice and PO-based payments for educational institutions worldwide. Contact support@zazatechnologies.com to request a quote.

**Q: How are School Licence seats managed?**  
A: School plans are seat-based. Admins can add/remove users via the Admin Dashboard. You can scale up or down at any time.

**Q: Can I cancel or pause my subscription?**  
A: Yes, you can cancel at any time via your billing settings. You’ll retain access until the end of your billing cycle.

---

## 🛠 FEATURES & FUNCTIONALITY

**Q: Can I edit or customise the AI-generated comments?**  
A: Yes! We strongly encourage it. Use AI as a foundation, then apply your professional judgment and personalise the output to reflect your student’s individuality.

**Q: Does it support multiple languages?**  
A: Yes. Zaza Promptly currently supports English, Spanish, French, German, and Italian, with more on the roadmap. We do not currently support Arabic or other languages beyond those listed.

**Q: Can I create my own comment templates?**  
A: Yes. On the Pro and School Licence plans, you can save, reuse, and share custom templates across your team.

**Q: Is curriculum alignment automatic?**  
A: Core subjects are automatically aligned to standards like the Australian Curriculum, US Common Core, UK National Curriculum, and IB. You can also upload your own framework via the Curriculum Mapping tool.

**Q: Can I bulk generate feedback?**  
A: Yes. With class-level input, Zaza can create differentiated comments for each student in one go. Available on Pro and School Licence.

---

## 📱 TECHNOLOGY & ACCESS

**Q: Do I need to install anything?**  
A: No installation required. Zaza Promptly is a browser-based app that works on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones.

**Q: What are the system/browser requirements?**  
A: We recommend Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest versions). Zaza is optimised for smooth performance on most modern devices.

**Q: Is there a native mobile app?**  
A: Yes! iOS and Android native apps are launching alongside our full platform rollout, so you’ll be able to use Zaza Promptly on the go.

**Q: Can I use Zaza on a Chromebook?**  
A: Absolutely. Chromebooks are fully supported as long as they run a modern browser.

---

## 🔐 PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE

**Q: What data do you store?**  
A: We don’t store personal student data. Input is processed in-session and not retained unless you explicitly save snippets or templates.

**Q: Is Zaza GDPR compliant?**  
A: Yes. We comply with GDPR, FERPA, and other key international privacy standards. Custom DPAs (Data Processing Agreements) are available on request.

**Q: Can I delete my data?**  
A: Yes. You can delete your snippets, account, and history at any time through your account settings.

---

## 💬 SUPPORT & TRAINING

**Q: How do I get help if something goes wrong?**  
A: You can contact us at support@zazatechnologies.com. Most support requests are answered within 24 hours. Pro and School users receive priority support.

**Q: Is training available for my team?**  
A: Yes. All School Licence plans include onboarding, live training sessions, and optional PD-style workshops. Pro users can request support on a case-by-case basis.

**Q: Do you have documentation or videos?**  
A: Yes. A full help centre with step-by-step guides, video walkthroughs, and quick tips is available at [zazateach.com/help](https://zazateach.com/help).
