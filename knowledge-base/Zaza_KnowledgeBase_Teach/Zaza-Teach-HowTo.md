
# 📘 Zaza Teach – User Guide & Best Practices

This guide helps educators get started with Zaza Teach and optimise their workflow for lesson planning, communication, and time-saving collaboration.

---

## ✅ Getting Started

1. **Sign Up**  
   Visit [zazateach.com](https://zazateach.com) and register using your school or personal email. Google single sign-on is also supported for faster access.

2. **Verify Your Email**  
   Check your inbox and confirm your email to activate your account. You’ll then be prompted to complete your profile.

3. **Set Your Preferences**  
   During onboarding, define your subject area, year level, teaching style, and tone preferences. This helps Zaza personalise your AI-generated lesson plans.

4. **Explore the Dashboard**  
   Navigate the intuitive dashboard with access to:
   - Lesson Library
   - Templates
   - Snippet Vault
   - AI Comment Generator
   - Settings & Personalisation

5. **Try Your First Lesson Plan**  
   Click “Create Lesson” to start a guided flow that generates your first plan in under 60 seconds.

---

## 🔄 Optimal Workflows

### ✏️ Standard Lesson Planning
1. **Input Context**: Enter 2–3 sentences about your learning goals, student profile, or curriculum standard.
2. **Choose Tone & Style**: Select from preset tones or define a custom voice.
3. **Click Generate**: The AI will return a structured draft lesson plan.
4. **Review & Refine**: Edit and personalise based on your classroom needs.
5. **Save or Export**: Add to your library or export to PDF/Word/Google Docs.

### 📦 Bulk Lesson Generation
1. **Prepare Class Data**: Use the provided spreadsheet template to enter learning objectives, topics, or student needs.
2. **Upload File**: Drag and drop your file into the Bulk Generator module.
3. **Set Preferences**: Choose tone, format (e.g. activity-based, inquiry-led), and student ability level.
4. **Generate in Batches**: AI produces multiple differentiated lesson plans.
5. **Final Review**: Review each for accuracy and coherence.
6. **Download or Share**: Export in desired formats for print, LMS, or collaboration.

### 👩‍👩‍👧 Parent Communication Made Easy
1. **Select a Scenario**: Choose from templates (e.g. academic update, behaviour note, celebration).
2. **Input Personalised Info**: Include student details, context, and desired outcome.
3. **Review Cultural Tone**: Ensure culturally responsive and inclusive language.
4. **Preview & Edit**: Fine-tune for clarity and professionalism.
5. **Send or Copy**: Email directly from the platform or copy to your communication tool.

---

## 💡 Expert Tips for Educators

- **🎯 Be Specific**: The more context you give (standards, student needs, goals), the better the output.
- **🧠 Use AI Like a Co-Teacher**: Think of Zaza as a draft partner — you remain the expert.
- **📁 Build a Reusable Library**: Save high-quality plans and comments for future reuse.
- **🗣 Maintain Consistent Tone**: Use the same voice across reports, feedback, and lessons.
- **💬 Use Snippets to Save Time**: Quickly insert recurring feedback, praise, or next steps.
