
# Zaza Teach – Use Cases

Zaza Teach helps educators at all levels streamline their lesson planning, reduce repetitive tasks, and focus on what really matters: quality teaching and student engagement. Below are detailed use cases to show how different teachers benefit from Zaza Teach.

---

## 🎯 Use Case 1: Year 4 Maths Teacher – Ms. Carter

**Before Zaza Teach:**  
Ms. Carter dreaded report and planning weeks. With 28 students and wide-ranging numeracy abilities, she often spent late nights rewording the same feedback for similar student needs. Creating differentiated lessons from scratch felt exhausting and repetitive.

**With Zaza Teach:**  
She now logs into Zaza Teach each Sunday, selects the Maths strand for fractions, identifies key focus areas (e.g., reasoning, number lines), and chooses from preloaded learning goals. With a few tweaks, Zaza creates a full lesson plan that includes objectives, activities, and differentiation.

**Results:**  
- Saves 4–5 hours weekly on lesson planning  
- Improved differentiation clarity  
- Feels less overwhelmed and more confident before Monday mornings

---

## 📘 Use Case 2: Secondary English Teacher – Mr. Dawson

**Before Zaza Teach:**  
Mr. Dawson taught six classes and juggled three texts at once. Planning lessons for Macbeth, persuasive writing, and film analysis across year levels was draining. He often reused outdated lesson plans or rushed through planning to meet deadlines.

**With Zaza Teach:**  
He enters key themes (e.g., ambition, guilt), selects the text (Macbeth), year level, and a focus (e.g., close reading, characterisation). Zaza generates a multi-phase lesson plan including warm-ups, guided questions, and group discussion prompts.

**Results:**  
- Gains 2 hours per day for marking and student feedback  
- Feels prepared and pedagogically sharper  
- Introduced creative tasks with AI-generated ideas he hadn't considered before

---

## 🌎 Use Case 3: Geography Relief Teacher – Ms. Singh

**Before Zaza Teach:**  
Called in last minute for Year 9 Humanities, Ms. Singh had no time to prepare and limited access to the school’s curriculum files. She improvised, but felt her lessons lacked coherence and flow.

**With Zaza Teach:**  
Using her phone, she inputs “climate change,” “sustainability,” and selects Year 9. Within 2 minutes, Zaza provides a full 60-minute lesson, with objectives, key terms, student activities, and a plenary.

**Results:**  
- Never unprepared, even at 30 minutes’ notice  
- Delivered high-quality content that impressed the class teacher  
- Reported feeling more empowered and less stressed

---

## 🧑‍🏫 Use Case 4: Special Education Teacher – Mr. Leung

**Before Zaza Teach:**  
Mr. Leung managed IEPs for 12 students and needed modified lessons for literacy and numeracy. Adapting general classroom materials was laborious and often took his whole evening.

**With Zaza Teach:**  
He sets learning goals to suit individual reading levels and selects visual-heavy or simplified formats. Zaza generates plans scaffolded with sentence starters, symbol support, and differentiated tasks.

**Results:**  
- Saves 6+ hours per week  
- Achieves IEP compliance effortlessly  
- Students are more engaged due to tailored content

## 🌐 Use Case 6: Multilingual Teacher in Europe – Mr. Ruiz

**Before Zaza Teach:**  
Teaching in English while supporting German and Spanish-speaking students, Mr. Ruiz struggled to create inclusive lessons. Translating worksheets and differentiating content was exhausting.

**With Zaza Teach:**  
He inputs lesson topics and toggles multilingual support. Zaza provides side-by-side English, German, and Spanish versions with consistent instructional quality.

- Lessons now accessible to all students  
- Parents praise the clear take-home summaries  
- Mr. Ruiz feels culturally responsive and supported
