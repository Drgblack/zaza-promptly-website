
# Zaza Teach – FAQ (Fully Expanded)

---

## 🧠 General Questions

**Q: Is Zaza Teach really free?**  
A: Yes. All users receive 5 free lesson plan generations per month with no credit card required. This allows educators to explore the platform and experience its value before deciding to upgrade. There are no hidden fees, and your free account includes access to basic templates, editing tools, and lesson saving functionality.

**Q: Is this just ChatGPT in disguise?**  
A: Not at all. While Zaza Teach leverages GPT-4, it is heavily constrained, fine-tuned, and curriculum-aligned. We’ve developed unique guardrails and pedagogical prompts that ensure lesson outputs are accurate, aligned with real classroom needs, and low in hallucination risk. This isn’t ChatGPT  -  it’s a purpose-built educator tool.

**Q: What happens when my trial or free plan limits are reached?**  
A: When your 5 monthly free generations are used, you'll receive a gentle prompt to upgrade to a paid plan. You won’t lose access to your saved lessons or preferences, but you won’t be able to generate new content until the next month or an upgrade.

**Q: Who is Zaza Teach for?**  
A: Zaza Teach is designed for K–12 educators, support teachers, curriculum leaders, and school administrators who want to reduce planning time, improve lesson quality, and embrace AI safely in the classroom.

## 💳 Billing & Licensing

**Q: Can I pay annually?**  
A: Yes. If you subscribe to the annual plan, you save 20% compared to monthly payments. It’s a popular option for individual teachers and schools budgeting yearly.

**Q: Can schools or districts pay by invoice or purchase order (PO)?**  
A: Absolutely. Our School Licence plans allow for invoice-based payments and purchase orders. We provide official quotes and contracts upon request and support custom billing cycles.

**Q: Are there account limits for School Licences?**  
A: Yes. Each School Licence includes a specific number of teacher accounts, with options to expand. You also get access to an admin dashboard for managing users, usage analytics, and template sharing across teams.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**  
A: Yes. Monthly subscribers can cancel anytime without penalty. Annual plans are billed upfront but remain active for the full year even if cancelled early.

## 🛠 Features & Functionality

**Q: Can I edit the AI-generated lesson plans?**  
A: Absolutely. Every lesson generated by Zaza Teach is fully editable. Teachers can tweak, extend, or reorganise the content before using it. We encourage you to treat AI output as a draft, not a final product.

**Q: Can I upload my own curriculum or standards?**  
A: Yes, with a Pro or School Licence. Our Curriculum Mapping tool allows you to link your local standards, topics, or assessment rubrics to the lesson builder, ensuring alignment.

**Q: Can I save and share templates with other teachers?**  
A: Yes. On the School Licence plan, users can share templates within their team and access a shared library of pre-vetted materials.

**Q: Can I reuse previous lessons or make small edits?**  
A: Yes. All lessons are saved to your personal library. You can duplicate, update, or remix them as needed  -  perfect for year-over-year use.

**Q: Does Zaza Teach support collaborative lesson planning?**  
A: Team features are included in the School Licence. Multiple teachers can co-create, comment, and version lesson plans within a shared workspace.

## ⚙️ Technical Questions

**Q: What are the minimum system requirements?**  
A: Zaza Teach works on any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari) and supports laptops, desktops, tablets, and most smartphones. No installation required.

**Q: Does Zaza Teach work on Chromebooks?**  
A: Yes. Many schools use Chromebooks, and Zaza Teach is fully optimised for them.

**Q: Is there a mobile app?**  
A: Not yet. A mobile app is in development. The current web app is fully responsive and works well on mobile browsers.

**Q: Can I use Zaza Teach offline?**  
A: No. At this time, an active internet connection is required to use the app and generate lesson content.

## 🔐 Privacy, Data & Compliance

**Q: Is student data stored or used to train AI?**  
A: No. Zaza Teach does not store or retain any identifiable student data. All data is processed temporarily in-session, and we do not use it for training or analytics.

**Q: Is Zaza Teach GDPR and FERPA compliant?**  
A: Yes. We are fully compliant with GDPR and align with FERPA principles. Our systems are designed with privacy-by-default protocols.

**Q: Can we sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA)?**  
A: Yes. Institutional users can request a DPA or custom agreement. Please contact support@zazatechnologies.com.

**Q: Where is data stored?**  
A: All data is securely stored in GDPR-compliant data centres located in the EU.
