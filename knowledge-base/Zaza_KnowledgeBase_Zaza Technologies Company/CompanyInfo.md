
# Company Information – Zaza Technologies

## Company Overview

Zaza Technologies UG (haftungsbeschränkt) is an AI-first educational software company based in Germany, dedicated to helping teachers thrive. We build privacy-conscious, emotionally aware, and pedagogically sound tools that save time, reduce burnout, and amplify creativity in the classroom.

## Mission

Empower educators through intelligent tools that support feedback, planning, creativity, communication, and wellbeing  -  without compromising teacher autonomy or values.

## Vision

To build the most trusted suite of AI-powered tools in education, rooted in empathy, effectiveness, and global impact.
