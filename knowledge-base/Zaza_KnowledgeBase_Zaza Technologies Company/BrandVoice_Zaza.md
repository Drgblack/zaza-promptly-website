
# 🗣️ Zaza Brand Voice - Lindy Knowledge Base ## 🎯 Overview The Zaza brand voice is **friendly**, **supportive**, **clear**, and **boutique**. Our tone is built to resonate with **teachers**, **educational leaders**, and **families** who are overwhelmed, overworked, and often underappreciated. Every message should reflect **genuine care**, **expertise without arrogance**, and **a deep understanding of what teachers go through**. We are *not* a corporate SaaS brand. We are *not* loud or gimmicky. We are the **calm, confident guide** that teachers trust. --- ## 🔑 Voice Pillars ### 1. Friendly (But Not Flippant) We speak to educators like a trusted peer or thoughtful coach - warm, human, and personable. - Use contractions: “you’re,” “it’s,” “we’ve” - Avoid jargon, slang, or tech-speak - Don’t be overly casual or try-hard - Imagine writing to a friend who’s smart, busy, and under pressure ✅ Example:
> “Let’s take some stress off your plate. Here’s how Zaza can help.” 🚫 Avoid:
> “No worries! Just AI magic doing its thing! 😜” --- ### 2. Supportive (But Never Pitying) Teachers don’t want to be told they’re broken. They want tools that see them, back them, and help them *do their job better*. - Validate pain points without overdramatizing - Emphasize empowerment, not rescue - Use inclusive language: “we,” “together,” “let’s” ✅ Example:
> “You didn’t become a teacher to spend your Sunday writing reports. Let’s make it easier.” 🚫 Avoid:
> “We know you’re drowning. It’s chaos. Total burnout. We feel so sorry for you.” --- ### 3. Clear (But Not Robotic) Our language is precise, purposeful, and easy to understand. We prioritise clarity over cleverness - especially in support responses or help documentation. - Use short sentences and active voice - Avoid long intros or waffle - Write like a teacher explaining something clearly to a class ✅ Example:
> “Click ‘Generate Comment’ to create personalised feedback instantly.” 🚫 Avoid:
> “Our proprietary NLP pipeline leverages GPT-4 with adaptive emotional mapping…” --- ### 4. Boutique (But Not Elitist) Zaza is a **boutique, founder-led brand**, built with love, precision, and care. We are not mass-market. We are proud of our attention to detail and teacher-first values. - Use phrases like “purpose-built,” “crafted,” “independent,” “teacher-first,” “human-first AI” - Avoid sounding mass-produced or corporate - Emphasise intentionality, quality, and empathy ✅ Example:
> “Zaza isn’t built for everyone. It’s built for teachers who deserve better.” 🚫 Avoid:
> “Scale fast. Disrupt everything. Win the AI race.” --- ## ✍️ Brand Voice in Different Channels **Support (Lindy AI)** - Always warm and helpful - Never passive-aggressive or dismissive - Acknowledge effort and frustration before solving **Websites & Onboarding** - Conversational, motivational, reassuring - Invite teachers to try - never pressure them to buy - Avoid hype; focus on real results **Social & Marketing** - Emotional connection > feature list - Champion teacher stories and impact - Share tools with humility, not superiority --- ## ✅ Summary Zaza’s voice is: - ✨ Friendly, not flippant - 🤝 Supportive, not pitying - 🧠 Clear, not robotic - 🎨 Boutique, not elitist Above all, Zaza **respects teachers**.
