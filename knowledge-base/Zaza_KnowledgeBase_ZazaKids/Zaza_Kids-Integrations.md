
# Zaza Kids – Integrations

Zaza Kids is designed with privacy, accessibility, and family-centered usage in mind. Our integration philosophy is based on supporting safe, enriching, and meaningful experiences for children, parents, and extended family members.

## 🌐 Core Integration Principles
- **Offline-First Experience**: 80% of Zaza Kids works without an internet connection, ensuring reliable access anywhere.
- **No Third-Party Ads or Trackers**: We do not integrate with any advertising or tracking platforms.
- **Privacy by Design**: All data remains encrypted, device-local when possible, and owned by the family.

---

## 🔗 Current Integrations

### 1. **Voice Assistant Sync**
- **Alexa and Google Assistant (Optional)**: Story reminders, drawing prompts, or bedtime narratives via voice-enabled devices.
- **Child-friendly design**: Requires parent opt-in and restricts data sharing.

### 2. **Smart Speaker Storytime**
- Listen to AI-generated heritage stories via speakers at bedtime.
- Customize story mood, tone, and language.

### 3. **Cloud Sync (Parental Control)**
- Families with multiple devices can sync across phones, tablets, and smart displays.
- End-to-end encryption ensures data remains private and shared only with approved devices.

### 4. **Drawing Scanner via Device Camera**
- Device-native camera integration supports easy drawing import.
- AI vision models process drawings locally to generate AR characters.

---

## 📅 Upcoming Integrations (Planned)
- **Apple Family Sharing & Google Family Library**: Support for shared family purchases and controls.
- **Digital Picture Frame Sync**: Export memory vault drawings to connected frames.
- **Library & School Archive Mode**: Optional learning archive sync for educators or cultural organisations.

---

## 🔐 No Educational LMS Integrations
Zaza Kids is **not** designed for school-based systems like Canvas or Google Classroom. It is a family-first platform focused on home learning, storytelling, emotional growth, and cultural preservation.
