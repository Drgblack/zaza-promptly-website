
# Zaza Kids – Policies & Commitments

Zaza Kids is built on a foundation of safety, transparency, cultural sensitivity, and child empowerment. These policies reflect our strong commitment to family-centred values and legal compliance.

---

## 🛡 Privacy & Data Protection

### ✅ COPPA & GDPR Compliant
- We collect minimal personal data.
- All features are designed with child privacy in mind.
- No data is ever sold or shared with third parties.

### 🔐 Data Ownership
- Families fully control all drawings, voice recordings, and stories.
- Memory Vault contents are encrypted and sharable only with family-authorised devices.

### ❌ No Ads, No Trackers
- We never show third-party ads.
- We do not use analytics platforms that track user behaviour.

---

## 🎨 Creative Freedom and Safety

### ✍️ Child-Created Content
- All AI-generated stories and AR characters are based on drawings submitted by the family.
- Tools ensure the drawings are transformed into respectful, culturally safe, emotionally supportive experiences.

### 🧠 AI Safeguards
- All content is moderated for age-appropriateness, emotional tone, and cultural sensitivity.
- Customizable filters available for families who want to further refine or restrict AI content.

---

## 🤝 Consent and Controls

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Dashboard
- Manage usage time, emotional wellbeing summaries, and device access.
- Enable/disable voice interactions, character animations, and cultural story themes.

### 📥 Download and Export Tools
- Families can export content into print-ready memory books, video timelines, or voice albums.
- Data exports are available on request and deletable at any time.

---

## 📆 Updates and Notification Policy

- Parents are notified in-app and via email when new features, languages, or story packs are released.
- All policy updates are shared at least 30 days in advance and require opt-in for sensitive changes.

---

Zaza Kids is built to protect families, empower children, and ensure joy, not judgement, in every interaction.
