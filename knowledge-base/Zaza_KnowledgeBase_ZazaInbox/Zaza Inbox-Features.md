
# Zaza Inbox – Expanded Features & Functionality

Zaza Inbox is designed to streamline teacher feedback, communication, and curriculum alignment through an intelligent, intuitive, and highly adaptable feature set. Below is an expanded overview of the platform’s capabilities:

---

## ✏️ Core Features

### 1. AI Comment Generator
**What It Does**:  
Zaza Inbox intelligently generates high-quality, personalized feedback for students within seconds. It uses your contextual input and adapts language and structure based on curriculum, tone, and audience.

**How It Works**:  
1. Enter 2–3 sentences describing student performance or context.  
2. Choose from multiple tone presets (see below).  
3. The AI crafts a polished, aligned comment.  
4. You review, refine if needed, and use immediately.  

**Why It Matters**:  
- Removes emotional and cognitive fatigue from repetitive writing.  
- Ensures clarity, consistency, and quality across all feedback.  
- Saves hours during busy reporting periods.  
- Empowers teachers to focus on teaching, not formatting.

---

### 2. Tone & Style Controls
**What It Does**:  
Allows full control over the emotional and professional tone of comments, making it ideal for everything from report cards to parent emails.

**Tones Available**:  
- **Professional** – For formal reports and external evaluations.  
- **Warm & Encouraging** – Builds student motivation.  
- **Constructive & Growth-Oriented** – Suggests actionable improvements.  
- **Collaborative** – Ideal for two-way parent–teacher communication.  
- **Academic** – Curriculum-anchored tone, useful in secondary and postsecondary contexts.

**Personalisation Options**:  
- Save and apply your preferred styles.  
- Align with school tone guides and policies.  
- Adjust for culturally appropriate phrasing or phrasing norms.  
- Create reusable tone templates for consistent usage.

---

### 3. Curriculum Alignment
**What It Does**:  
Automatically maps your comment to curriculum standards, learning goals, and assessment rubrics.

**Curricula Covered**:  
- UK National Curriculum  
- Common Core (USA)  
- Australian Curriculum  
- Custom school-specific frameworks (via upload)

**Benefits**:  
- Minimises risk of off-target or vague feedback.  
- Promotes transparency with parents and students.  
- Reinforces learning objectives clearly and confidently.

---

### 4. Snippet Library
**What It Does**:  
Lets teachers build a personalised library of reusable comment structures and phrases.

**Key Features**:  
- Sort snippets by subject, tone, or theme.  
- Custom tags make retrieval fast and intuitive.  
- Share snippets with your team (Pro+ plans).  
- Auto-suggests relevant snippets during comment generation.

**Why It Helps**:  
- Builds your own efficiency engine.  
- Reduces burnout from writing the same thing repeatedly.  
- Maintains professional consistency across reports.

---

### 5. Multi-language Support
**What It Does**:  
Enables multilingual comment creation for diverse classrooms and global teacher teams.

**Supported Languages (Initial Release)**:  
- English  
- Spanish  
- French  
- German  
- Italian  
- More to come (requestable via support)

**Cultural Intelligence Layer**:  
- Adapts idioms and phrasing to local expectations.  
- Supports language-appropriate tone shifts.  
- Includes formal/informal toggle where applicable.

---

## ⚙️ Advanced Features

### 6. Bulk Comment Generation
- Upload a CSV or connect to your SIS.  
- Process 10–100+ students at once.  
- Preserve tone and contextual accuracy per student.  
- Export all as PDF, CSV, or push to Google Classroom.

### 7. Custom Curriculum Mapping
- Upload your school’s curriculum standards.  
- Train Zaza Inbox to align with your grading language.  
- Map snippets or full comments to school rubrics.  
- Useful for IEPs, private institutions, and bilingual curricula.

### 8. Analytics Dashboard
- See how much time you’ve saved.  
- Track usage by subject, class, or tone.  
- Identify common feedback areas.  
- Spot teaching patterns and opportunities.

### 9. Export Options
- PDF for formal reports.  
- CSV for gradebook imports.  
- Rich text for LMS or email pasting.  
- Direct sync with Google Docs, Seesaw, and Microsoft Teams.

## 🚫 Known Limitations

- Mobile app in development; mobile web view is responsive but lacks offline mode.  
- Snippet sharing requires school-wide licence.  
- Curriculum alignment with custom rubrics may need validation.  
- Does not include lesson planning or visual resource generation (see Zaza Teach or Zaza Visuals).

---

## Summary

Zaza Inbox is not just a feedback tool. It’s a teacher’s virtual assistant - one that understands your tone, aligns with your curriculum, saves your time, and empowers your professional voice.
