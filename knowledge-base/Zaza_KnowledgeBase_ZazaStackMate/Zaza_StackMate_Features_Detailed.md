# Zaza StackMate - Features Zaza StackMate is your personal AI-powered assistant designed for non-technical professionals, educators, freelancers, and solopreneurs. It streamlines your workflows, reduces cognitive load, and empowers you to focus on meaningful tasks. Below is a detailed overview of its core features. --- ## 🧠 AI-Driven Workflows Zaza StackMate automates repetitive and cognitive-heavy workflows through intelligent templates and AI orchestration. Users can: - Create, modify, and execute workflow templates for tasks such as onboarding, outreach, SOP generation, and coaching plans. - Build and save reusable frameworks tailored to personal, team, or client needs. - Trigger workflows using natural language, dropdown selectors, or integrated schedule cues. --- ## 🧩 Smart Blocks Smart Blocks are modular, context-aware elements that act as building blocks for complex documents or tasks. - Include blocks like “About Me”, “Client Profile”, “Value Proposition”, “Next Steps”, and more. - Combine blocks into dynamic documents (e.g., sales proposals, lesson plans, onboarding journeys). - Each block adapts based on the overall tone, audience, and goal of the workflow. --- ## 🎨 Mascot Prompt Templates Every workflow comes with a customizable mascot template that guides and contextualizes the AI responses. - Mascots shape the voice and tone of outputs (e.g., friendly mentor, precise analyst). - Choose a mascot per task to influence emotional resonance and user alignment. - Great for educators creating warm feedback, coaches writing motivational plans, or consultants drafting professional summaries. --- ## 📦 Saved Stacks Users can bundle workflows, documents, and templates into “Stacks”: - Reuse project structures across clients or campaigns. - Share stacks with collaborators or clients. - Include role-based instructions, custom prompts, or integrations. --- ## 🔄 Context Switching & Adaptive Memory Zaza StackMate intelligently maintains context across sessions: - Tracks user goals, project types, and usage patterns. - Adapts outputs based on current tone (e.g., calm vs. energetic) and previously selected styles. - Allows seamless task switching without losing continuity. --- ## 🧭 Guided Discovery A built-in assistant helps users: - Choose the right workflow or stack based on intent. - Adjust tone, audience type, and delivery style. - Receive live examples and AI suggestions mid-task. --- ## 🗂️ Document Assembly & Export Export final results from StackMate in various formats: - Download full reports, documents, and responses as PDF, DOCX, or markdown. - Preview outputs in real-time as they are generated. - Batch-generate similar documents using variant inputs (e.g., one plan for each team member). --- ## 🌐 Multilingual Support Zaza StackMate supports multiple languages to enable global usage: - Create workflows and documents in English, German, Spanish, French, and Italian. - Switch languages at any point in the process. - Useful for international educators, coaches, or service providers. --- ## 🔐 Role & Tone Personalisation Users can define the voice of their work: - Choose from tones like empathetic, authoritative, concise, or playful. - Customize role profiles such as “Instructional Designer”, “Coach”, “Consultant”, or “Team Leader”. - AI adapts content based on selected combinations of tone and role. --- ## 🧩 Coming Soon -
