# Zaza StackMate – Data & Privacy Policies

Your privacy is important. We design every part of StackMate with simplicity, transparency, and user control in mind.

## 🔐 Data Privacy

- We **do not collect sensitive personal information**
- No student, classroom, or educational records are processed
- All data remains within the EU unless explicitly consented otherwise

## 🧠 AI Interactions

- Conversations with mascots are stored only to improve your session experience
- You can **delete all saved sessions and preferences at any time**
- We don’t use your prompts or project details for training external models

## 🌍 Compliance

- GDPR compliant (no FERPA relevance as this is not for educational institutions)
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are available on all StackMate product pages
