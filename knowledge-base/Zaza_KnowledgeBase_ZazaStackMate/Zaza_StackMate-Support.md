# Zaza StackMate – Support

Whether you're a coach, educator, freelancer, or solopreneur, Zaza StackMate is designed to empower you to build your own AI-powered tools. We're here to support your journey every step of the way.

---

## 🧰 Help & Support Channels

- **In-App Assistant ("Mascot")**  
  Your AI companion delivers context-aware support tailored to your current stack and build phase.

- **Smart Documentation**  
  Step-by-step guides and walkthroughs that adapt to your goals and experience level.

- **Live Chat (Beta)**  
  Available in-app weekdays 9am–6pm CET for quick questions and guidance.

- **Email Support**  
  Reach us directly at [support@zazateach.com](mailto:support@zazateach.com)

- **Community Discord**  
  Connect with other builders, share ideas, and learn from community projects. Great for coaches, creators, and first-time AI builders.

- **Help Centre**  
  Full documentation, FAQs, and product update notes available at: https://support.zazastackmate.com

## ⚙️ Common Issues & Quick Fixes

- **Can't create new agent?**  
  → Check your prompt structure and ensure your plan includes agent creation.

- **Agent not behaving as expected?**  
  → Use **Test Mode** to fine-tune your instructions and role logic.

- **Missing a feature?**  
  → Contact support to request features or explore plan upgrades.

## 🔒 What StackMate Doesn’t Support

To keep our scope focused and effective, we do not offer:

- Live classroom walkthroughs  
- Grading or assessment support  
- Student or LMS-focused integrations  
- Educational compliance features like FERPA
