
# Zaza StackMate – How-To Guide

Welcome to **Zaza StackMate**, your intelligent sidekick for building AI workflows, creating content, and organizing your tech stack—even if you're not a coder. This guide walks you through how to use Zaza StackMate effectively from setup to advanced usage.

---

## 🔧 Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up and Log In
- Visit [zazastackmate.ai](https://zazastackmate.ai) and create a free account.
- Use Google, Apple, or email authentication to sign in.

### 2. Create Your First Project
- Click **“New Project”** on the dashboard.
- Choose your project type:
  - Personal Productivity
  - Business Workflow
  - Content Automation
  - Client Support
- Name your project and click **“Create”**.

---

## 🧠 Build Your AI Stack

### 3. Add a StackMate (Your AI Mascot)
- Select from mascot templates (e.g. CoachBuddy, BizGenie, IdeaWhiz) or create your own.
- Customize tone, role, purpose, and example responses.
- Click **“Activate Mascot”**.

### 4. Add Your Tools
- Integrate apps like:
  - Notion, Slack, Gmail, Trello
  - Google Docs, Sheets
  - Custom webhook or API endpoints
- Authorize access to fetch tasks, content, or documents.

---

## 🗃️ Organise Your Workflows

### 5. Use a Template
- Choose from curated StackMate templates for:
  - Weekly planning
  - Social content calendars
  - Client onboarding
  - Workflow automation for solopreneurs

### 6. Customize with Blocks
- Drag in modular blocks like:
  - ✏️ Content Generator
  - 📋 Task Parser
  - 📁 Document Summarizer
  - 💡 Prompt Refiner
- Stack blocks visually to create a flow (like a no-code builder).

---

## ⚙️ Use Your StackMate

### 7. Run Conversations or Flows
- Click into your StackMate.
- Ask it questions like:
  - “Summarise this Google Doc.”
  - “Draft an onboarding email for a new HR hire.”
  - “What’s trending in productivity newsletters?”
- Watch the AI handle context, file fetching, and logic.

---

## 💾 Save, Share, Export

### 8. Save as Preset
- Save your entire flow or custom StackMate as a **Preset**.
- Share it with your team or community using a public link.

### 9. Export Results
- Export outputs as:
  - .md, .pdf, .docx
  - Direct push to Notion or Google Docs
  - Zapier/Make automation trigger

---

## 🔍 Advanced Tips

- Enable **Memory Mode** to store preferences and conversation history.
- Use **Command Tags** like `#summarize`, `#compare`, `#explain` to prompt faster results.
- Schedule flows to run weekly or daily using automation settings.

---

## 🧑‍💼 Best for:
- Non-technical creators
- SMB owners
- Freelancers
- Coaches & consultants
- Operations & HR teams
