# Zaza StackMate - Troubleshooting Guide This guide provides solutions to common issues users might encounter while using Zaza StackMate. If your issue isn't covered here, please reach out via the Support section. --- ## 🚫 I can’t log in to my StackMate account ### Possible Causes: - Incorrect email or password - No internet connection - Temporary service outage ### Solutions:
1. Double-check your email and password for typos.
2. Try resetting your password using the “Forgot Password” link.
3. Ensure your internet connection is active.
4. Visit [status.zazatechnologies.com](https://status.zazatechnologies.com) to check for known outages. --- ## 🧠 My AI assistant isn’t responding ### Possible Causes: - Server-side lag - Conversation session timeout - Exceeded request limit ### Solutions:
1. Refresh your browser and try again.
2. Clear your cache or use an incognito window.
3. Wait a few minutes - there may be a temporary delay on our side.
4. Check if you've exceeded your usage tier limit under **Settings > Account Plan**. --- ## 🔄 StackMate isn’t saving my workflows or configurations ### Possible Causes: - Unsaved changes - Browser storage issues - Backend sync error ### Solutions:
1. Ensure you click **Save** before exiting a configuration screen.
2. Try using a different browser or device.
3. Log out and back in to refresh session sync. --- ## 🔗 Third-party tool integration isn't working ### Possible Causes: - Invalid API keys - Permission issues with third-party services - Integration in beta or currently unsupported ### Solutions:
1. Recheck that you’ve pasted correct and valid API keys under **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Ensure the third-party app allows external API access.
3. Consult the [Integration Guide](https://zazatechnologies.com/stackmate/integrations) for setup steps and compatibility notes. --- ## ⚠️ Error messages during content generation ### Common Errors: - “Prompt too long” - “Context expired” - “Quota exceeded” ### Fixes:
1. Break long prompts into smaller parts.
2. Refresh the page and start a new session.
3. Upgrade your plan if you’ve reached the free tier’s content cap. --- ## 📂 My saved workflows are missing ### Solutions: - Make sure you are logged into the correct email account. - Check the “Archived” section under **Workflows**. - Contact support if deletion was accidental - we may be able to restore your data. --- ## 🔍 I can’t find a specific feature I saw in the documentation ### Solutions: - Some features may be in staged rollout. If your account doesn’t have access yet, contact support to request early access. - Use the search bar at the top right to find tools, commands, and saved snippets. - Double-check which workspace or view you’re currently in - features may be workspace-specific. --- ## 🛠 Still stuck?
