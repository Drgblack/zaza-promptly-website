# Zaza StackMate – Integrations

StackMate integrates with a growing list of platforms and tools to expand your workflow automation capabilities:

## Available Integrations

- **Gmail**: Auto-reply agents, meeting follow-ups, smart drafts.
- **Google Drive**: Organize and reference documents within prompts.
- **Notion**: Create, query, and edit documents from AI workflows.
- **Slack**: Trigger agents with messages, route replies automatically.
- **Zapier / Make**: Connect StackMate to 1000+ other apps.
- **Calendly**: Auto-generate follow-ups and meeting notes.

## Planned Integrations

- Stripe (invoicing agents)  
- LinkedIn (content agents)  
- Airtable (CRM automation)
