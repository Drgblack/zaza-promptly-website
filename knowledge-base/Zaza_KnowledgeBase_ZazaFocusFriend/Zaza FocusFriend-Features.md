# Zaza FocusFriend – Features & Functionality (Expanded Edition)

Zaza FocusFriend is designed as a stress-relieving writing companion for teachers. Its mission is to streamline the emotionally and cognitively draining process of generating professional, effective, and parent-sensitive feedback — so teachers can focus on what they do best: teaching.

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. AI-Powered Comment Generator

**What It Does:**  
Transforms short contextual notes into full, polished feedback comments in under 30 seconds.

**How It Works:**  
1. Teacher enters 1–3 sentences about the student’s behaviour, achievement, or challenges.
2. Selects a tone/style (e.g. warm, formal, honest-but-gentle).
3. The AI writes a full comment aligned to curriculum and tone.
4. Teacher edits, saves, or copies the comment into their reports/email.

**Why It Matters:**  
- Banishes blank-page syndrome and comment fatigue.
- Saves up to 10 hours per report cycle.
- Offers linguistic polish and differentiation for every student.
- Avoids repetitive phrasing across a cohort.

---

### 2. Tone, Style & Empathy Controls

**Tone Presets Include:**  
- Professional and formal  
- Warm and nurturing  
- Constructive and strength-based  
- Collaborative (for home-school partnership)  
- Academic subject-specific (STEM, Arts, Humanities)  

**Customisation:**  
- Save school-specific or personal tone preferences.
- Adjust tone dynamically for parent communications or sensitive cases.
- Fine-tune phrasing for cultural nuances or linguistic needs (ESL/EAL friendly).

**Why Teachers Love This:**  
- Helps navigate emotionally complex reporting.
- Ensures the right balance of honesty, encouragement, and clarity.
- Makes teachers feel supported when writing tough feedback.

---

### 3. Curriculum & Assessment Alignment

**Built-In Coverage:**  
- National curriculum standards (AU/UK/NZ/US/CA and more)
- Subject-specific learning outcomes
- Formative and summative assessment criteria

**Advanced Mapping:**  
- Upload and map your school’s internal frameworks
- Auto-suggest criteria-aligned phrases
- Built-in links to learning progressions

**Why It Matters:**  
- Ensures compliance with reporting expectations.
- Strengthens instructional alignment.
- Speaks the “language” your leadership teams understand.

---

### 4. Snippet Library & Feedback Bank

**What It Does:**  
Lets teachers save, organise, and reuse powerful comment snippets or frameworks.

**Features:**  
- Tag snippets by subject, tone, or student type (e.g. high-achieving, struggling, disengaged)
- Use suggested snippets from trusted templates
- Create personal or school-wide libraries (School Licence required)
- Access snippet history across sessions

**Value:**  
- Builds long-term efficiency and voice consistency.
- Fosters collaboration across year levels or subject teams.
- Reduces decision fatigue during report season.

---

### 5. Multilingual & Culturally Responsive Feedback

**Languages Supported:**  
- English (UK/US), Spanish, French, German, Italian — more on request.

**Features:**  
- Translate comments for multilingual families.
- Automatically adjust tone to fit cultural expectations.
- Maintain idiomatic clarity while preserving educational meaning.

**Why This Matters:**  
- Improves equity in communication.
- Supports international or diverse school communities.
- Helps ESL/EAL teachers save time and reduce errors.

---

## 🚀 Advanced Features

### Bulk Comment Generation

Generate feedback for entire classes in a batch process while maintaining individual tone and customisation. Includes:
- Import student lists
- Auto-fill contextual data (e.g. subject or term)
- Export all in one file (PDF, CSV, or SIS formats)

---

### Custom Curriculum Mapping

Upload your own school standards or unique program criteria (e.g. IB, Montessori). Use these to:
- Generate tailored feedback
- Track alignment over time
- Ensure reports meet internal frameworks

---

### Analytics & Reflection Dashboard

Track your usage trends and impact:
- See how many hours saved per report cycle
- Identify which snippets or tones are used most often
- Get insights for professional reflection or appraisal

---

### Exports & Sharing

- Export to PDF or CSV for school submission.
- Copy-paste into email or reports with formatting preserved.
- Integration-ready for major SIS platforms (Compass, Edval, etc.).

---

## 🔌 Integrations

Zaza FocusFriend is built for your stack.

- **Student Information Systems (SIS):** Import data directly  
- **Google Classroom:** Embed feedback into assignments  
- **Microsoft Teams:** Use comments in student progress chats  
- **Seesaw:** Add to learning portfolios  
- **API Access:** For custom school integrations (School Plan only)  

---

## 🚧 Known Limitations (as of July 2025)

- Mobile PWA available, but no native iOS/Android app (coming Q4 2025).
- Snippet sharing requires School Licence tier.
- Custom curriculum uploads require manual validation before auto-alignment.
- Not intended for lesson generation or visual content (see Zaza Teach or Visuals).

---

Zaza FocusFriend is built to empower teachers, reduce burnout, and elevate the quality of student–parent communication. The more you use it, the more powerful it becomes.
