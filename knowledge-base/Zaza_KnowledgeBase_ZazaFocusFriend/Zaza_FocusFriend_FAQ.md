
# Zaza FocusFriend – Comprehensive FAQ

## 🧠 General Questions

**Q: What exactly is Zaza FocusFriend?**  
A: Zaza FocusFriend is your personalised AI-powered productivity companion designed for teachers and students. It blends behavioural science, educational psychology, and curriculum-aligned GPT-4 logic to boost attention, motivation, and executive functioning. Unlike generic AI tools, FocusFriend is built to scaffold real academic progress through smart nudges, feedback cycles, and habit reinforcement.

**Q: Is Zaza FocusFriend really free?**  
A: Yes! All users receive 5 free content generations per month. This includes AI-generated focus plans, goal prompts, and feedback templates. After the limit, you can upgrade or wait until your monthly limit resets.

**Q: How is this different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?**  
A: FocusFriend is not just a wrapper around GPT. It’s fine-tuned with educational constraints and goal-matching architecture that avoids hallucinations, focuses on bite-sized actionable planning, and speaks in motivational tones crafted for real classroom and study environments.

**Q: What happens after my trial or free quota ends?**  
A: You’ll be notified when your quota is near its limit. Once it’s used up, you’ll still have access to your saved focus prompts and templates, but generation capabilities will be paused until renewal or upgrade.

## 💳 Billing & Licences

**Q: What are the pricing tiers?**  
A: We offer three options:
- Free: 5 generations/month.
- Pro Plan: Unlimited access, extra tone options, and personal focus analytics.
- School Licence: Multi-seat access, admin dashboards, team libraries, and live training.

**Q: Can I pay annually and get a discount?**  
A: Absolutely. Annual plans receive a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.

**Q: Do you accept school purchase orders or bank transfer payments?**  
A: Yes, we support POs and direct invoices for educational institutions under our School Licence programme.

**Q: How many users are included in a School Licence?**  
A: Our School Licence scales with your team size. You’ll choose the number of seats, and admin tools allow for easy user management, SSO integration, and usage reporting.

## 🚀 Features & Functionality

**Q: What can I use Zaza FocusFriend for?**  
A: You can:
- Generate personalised focus plans for lessons or study sessions.
- Craft motivational AI messages for students.
- Build habit loops and checkpoint goals for learning.
- Write progress summaries and reflections with the AI.
- Share focus templates across your school.

**Q: Can I customise AI-generated plans?**  
A: Definitely. All suggestions can be edited before saving or sharing. FocusFriend is a co-pilot, not a replacement for your professional judgement.

**Q: Can I upload or map my own curriculum?**  
A: Yes, with Pro and School plans, you can integrate your curriculum via our Curriculum Mapping tool. This allows the AI to better align focus suggestions and vocabulary with your actual learning objectives.

**Q: Does it support template sharing?**  
A: Yes, School Licence users can create, save, and share reusable templates. This is ideal for departments or grade-level teams who want to standardise focus plans.

**Q: What’s the difference between a Focus Prompt and a Focus Cycle?**  
A: A Focus Prompt is a quick motivational or planning interaction with the AI. A Focus Cycle is a longer scaffolded journey, guiding users from intention-setting to reflection using checkpoints, reminders, and tone adaptation.

## 🖥️ Technical Requirements

**Q: What devices can I use FocusFriend on?**  
A: FocusFriend is a fully responsive web app. It works on:
- Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari browsers.
- Windows, Mac, Linux, Chromebooks.
- Tablets and smartphones.

**Q: Is there a native mobile app?**  
A: Not yet, but it’s on our roadmap. The web app is already mobile-optimised and functions like a native app when saved to home screen.

**Q: Is it accessible offline?**  
A: Some features (like saved templates and notes) are accessible offline via local caching. AI generation requires an internet connection.

## 🔐 Privacy & Data Compliance

**Q: What data does Zaza FocusFriend store?**  
A: We do not store student names, grades, or personally identifiable data unless explicitly submitted in template creation. All generation inputs are processed in-session and anonymised.

**Q: Is FocusFriend GDPR and FERPA compliant?**  
A: Yes. We adhere to strict data processing and storage policies. For institutions, we offer signed DPAs upon request.

**Q: Will my school data be secure?**  
A: Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Institutional users have access to role-based permissions and audit logs.

## 📞 Support & Training

**Q: How do I get help if something isn’t working?**  
A: You can contact us anytime at support@zazatechnologies.com. We reply within 24 hours on weekdays. Pro and School users receive priority response.

**Q: Can we get live onboarding or team training?**  
A: Yes. Live virtual sessions are included in the School Licence and available as an add-on for Pro users. We also provide PDF guides, Loom tutorials, and email walkthroughs.

**Q: Is there a way to suggest new features?**  
A: Absolutely! You can submit feedback through the app’s “Suggest a Feature” tab or email us directly. Many of our features come directly from teacher requests.
