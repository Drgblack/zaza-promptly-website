# Zaza Schwoop – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Zaza Schwoop is a mood-adaptive, AI-powered study app designed to help students learn better based on how they feel. Below are frequently asked questions to help you understand how Schwoop works, who it's for, and why it’s unlike any other study tool.

---

### ❓ What is Zaza Schwoop?

Zaza Schwoop is a mobile-first, emotionally intelligent study app that helps students learn more effectively by adapting to their mood. It uses AI to generate flashcards, create fun quiz battles, and drive motivation through mood-based study modes and social learning dynamics.

---

### 🎯 Who is Zaza Schwoop for?

Schwoop is designed for:
- **Students (ages 13–25)**: Especially high school, university, and exam-prep learners.
- **Teachers**: Who want insights into how students revise and stay engaged.
- **Parents**: Who want to encourage healthy, productive study habits.

---

### 🌀 How does Schwoop adapt to a student’s mood?

Schwoop offers mood-based study modes like:
- **Chill Mode** – Low-stress study mode with soft tones and paced flashcards.
- **Focus Mode** – Structured and distraction-free interface for deep learning.
- **Cram Mode** – High-intensity mode for rapid revision under pressure.

The app adapts UI visuals, audio cues, pacing, and flashcard phrasing based on selected vibes.

---

### 📚 What kind of content does Schwoop generate?

Schwoop uses GPT-4 to:
- Generate flashcards from pasted notes, textbook photos, or uploaded files.
- Summarise large blocks of text into quiz decks instantly.
- Suggest related topics and study prompts based on the user’s subject and vibe.

---

### 🎮 What are Quiz Battles?

Quiz Battles let students:
- Challenge themselves (solo XP mode).
- Compete with friends in head-to-head duels.
- Earn points, streaks, and custom badges.
- Share “Schwoop Replays” to show off wins and study highlights.

---

### 🤝 What is Study Squads?

Study Squads are small learning groups within Schwoop:
- Up to 6 friends per squad.
- Shared decks, chat, leaderboards.
- Collective streaks and accountability boosts.
- Great for exam prep, class-based study, or group learning projects.

---

### 🛒 What is the Deck Marketplace?

The Deck Marketplace is a space where:
- Students can access high-quality, community-made decks.
- Top creators can monetise their content (earn 70% revenue share).
- Teachers can curate decks for class use and share them directly with students.

---

### 🔐 How does Schwoop protect student data?

Schwoop is FERPA/GDPR aligned and:
- Requires no personal identifiers for basic use.
- Offers school-admin dashboards for safe classroom integration.
- Never shares private decks or mood data without explicit consent.

---

### 🔗 Can Schwoop connect with Zaza Teach?

Yes. In future updates:
- Teachers can export lesson topics from Zaza Teach as Schwoop decks.
- Analytics can show study activity linked to planned lessons.
- Students can sync their mood-based study outcomes back to teachers.

---

### 🧠 Does Schwoop actually help students learn?

Yes. Schwoop improves:
- **Retention** through spaced repetition and adaptive tone.
- **Engagement** with interactive and gamified mechanics.
- **Motivation** by reducing study stress and celebrating progress.

Pilot testing shows improved recall and user-reported productivity increases of 30–45%.

---

### 📈 What makes Schwoop different from Quizlet, Anki, or Brainscape?

Unlike traditional flashcard apps:
- Schwoop **adapts to emotion**, not just topic.
- Offers **AI-powered creation** in seconds, not hours.
- Includes **battles, streaks, social sharing**, and more.
- Is part of the **Zaza ecosystem**, bridging students and teachers.

---

Not yet. API access is on the roadmap for Q3 2026, focused on school dashboards and Zaza ecosystem integration.
