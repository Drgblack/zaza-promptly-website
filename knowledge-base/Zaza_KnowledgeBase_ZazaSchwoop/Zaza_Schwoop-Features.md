# Zaza Schwoop – Features & Functionality

Zaza Schwoop is a dynamic, emotionally adaptive tool that helps teachers write student feedback and progress reports faster, clearer, and with more confidence. Below is an in-depth look at what makes it a powerful part of the Zaza ecosystem.

---

## 🔑 Core Features

### 1. **AI-Powered Comment Generator**
**What It Does:** Automatically generates personalised, professional student feedback based on a few sentences of input.

**How It Works:**
- Teachers enter 2–3 lines of context per student (e.g. academic performance, behaviour, goals).
- Choose desired tone and communication style.
- Zaza generates high-quality, curriculum-aligned comments in seconds.
- Teacher can review, fine-tune, and export instantly.

**Why It Matters:**
- Cuts report-writing time by up to 80%
- Helps overcome writer’s block and mental fatigue
- Guarantees tone-consistency and alignment with standards

---

### 2. **Tone & Style Personalisation**
**Tones Available:**
- ✅ Professional and formal
- ✅ Warm and encouraging
- ✅ Constructive and developmental
- ✅ Collaborative (for parents)
- ✅ Academic subject-specific

**Customisation Options:**
- Upload tone guides or preferred phrases
- Save teacher/school-specific presets
- Adjust for cultural context and sensitivity

**Outcome:** Comments sound authentically you while meeting school policy requirements.

---

### 3. **Curriculum & Assessment Alignment**
**Aligned With:**
- National education standards
- Subject-specific learning objectives
- Localised curriculum requirements

**Advanced Capabilities:**
- Upload your school’s internal framework
- Map feedback directly to rubrics
- Suggest next steps or goals for progression

**Impact:** Feedback is not just fast - it’s meaningful, traceable, and evidence-based.

---

### 4. **Reusable Snippet Library**
**What You Can Do:**
- Save your best feedback lines or structures
- Tag and organise them by subject, year level, tone, or skill area
- Instantly reuse proven phrases for multiple students

**Why It Works:** Saves mental energy, improves quality over time, and builds a bank of professional language.

---

### 5. **Multi-Language Support**
**Languages Covered:**
- English (default)
- Spanish, French, German, Italian
- More languages rolling out

**Features:**
- Localisation-aware phrasing
- Accurate educational translations
- Respectful of parent/caregiver cultural norms

**Use Case:** Communicate with diverse communities in their preferred language.

---

## 💼 Advanced Features

### 🔃 Bulk Comment Generation
- Process an entire class in minutes
- Maintains personalisation per student
- Exports in PDF, CSV, or SIS-compatible formats

### 🧩 Custom Curriculum Mapping
- Align comments with internal school rubrics
- Set indicators for IEPs, behaviour goals, or enrichment programs

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard (Pro/School only)
- Time saved per report cycle
- Feedback patterns per subject or year
- Insights for teacher PD and training

### 📤 Export Options
- PDF, Word, CSV
- Copy to clipboard
- Push to SIS, Google Classroom, Seesaw, or Microsoft Teams

## ⚠️ Known Limitations (as of July 2025)

- No native iOS/Android app yet (mobile PWA available)
- Snippet library sharing is only for School Licence plans
- Curriculum auto-alignment is strongest with major English-speaking standards (others may require adjustment)
- Focuses only on feedback, not lesson planning or visual aids (see Zaza Teach or Visuals)
