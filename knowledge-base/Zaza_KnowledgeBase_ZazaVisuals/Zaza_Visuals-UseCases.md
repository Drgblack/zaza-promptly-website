# Zaza Visuals - Expanded Use Case Examples Zaza Visuals is a dynamic AI-powered tool built to serve the diverse needs of teachers, support staff, and school leaders across educational levels. Below are enhanced and expanded use cases showcasing how different educators can integrate Zaza Visuals into their daily workflow to maximise impact, save time, and enhance communication quality. --- ## 🎯 Use Case 1: Year 4 Maths Teacher - Personalised Report Writing at Scale ### Problem
End-of-term reports require writing 28 individualised student reflections. Balancing accuracy, personalisation, and tone consistency is a major time sink - often requiring 10+ hours across a weekend. ### Zaza Visuals Solution - Upload or link a spreadsheet with student performance data (e.g. test scores, teacher notes). - Input targeted phrases such as: - “Frequently struggles with fractions” - “Demonstrates strong problem-solving skills” - “Exceeds expectations in geometry” - Select a tone preset: **Academic**, **Supportive**, or **Growth-Oriented** - Auto-generate full report text for each student - Export to PDF or copy to school reporting system ### Outcome
✅ Maintains a consistent voice across all reports ✅ Reflects each student’s progress accurately ✅ Frees up ~4.5 hours of teacher time per report cycle ✅ Reduces cognitive overload during high-pressure reporting periods --- ## 📬 Use Case 2: Secondary English Teacher - Parent Communication Made Simple ### Problem
Weekly emails to parents about behaviour and learning progress are emotionally draining and repetitive. Striking the right balance between honesty and encouragement is difficult. ### Zaza Visuals Solution - Choose the **Parent Communication** preset - Jot down quick notes such as: - “Often distracted in class” - “Recent improvements in essay structure” - “Needs support with time management” - Choose tone: **Encouraging**, **Firm but Fair**, or **Collaborative** - Instantly generate parent-facing email content ### Outcome
✅ Clear, professional language every time ✅ Improved parental understanding and engagement ✅ Reduces teacher anxiety about difficult conversations ✅ Saves ~15-30 minutes per student per week --- ## 🧩 Use Case 3: SEN Support Staff - Daily Log & Feedback Records ### Problem
SEN documentation requires tracking differentiated feedback and progress notes daily for multiple students. Writing unique feedback for each is mentally draining and time-consuming. ### Zaza Visuals Solution - Use the **Snippet Library** to preload common feedback sentences by support area (e.g. speech, focus, motor skills) - Add a few quick notes for each student session - Let Zaza Visuals generate full log entries with consistent voice and tone - Export to school system or archive for team reviews ### Outcome
✅ Higher-quality, standardised documentation ✅ Maintains tone and detail across multiple staff members ✅ Reduces daily writing burden significantly ✅ Enables better longitudinal analysis of progress --- ## ✏️ Use Case 4: Deputy Principal - School-Wide Communication Templates ### Problem
Crafting whole-school communication, newsletter blurbs, or policy announcements that sound human and well-phrased without taking hours. ### Zaza Visuals Solution - Choose **School Announcements** tone - Input a few points or bullet notes - Let the tool generate a professional, warm message suitable for stakeholders - Translate into multiple languages if needed ### Outcome
✅ Saves hours on each school-wide message ✅ Reduces dependency on one communicator ✅ Ensures consistency across internal and external messaging --- ## 🧠 Use Case 5: New Teacher - Weekly Classroom Reflections ### Problem
New teachers struggle with articulating weekly classroom reflections or mentor updates, leading to inconsistent or underdeveloped reporting. ### Zaza Visuals Solution - Choose the **Teacher Reflections** preset - Add notes on weekly goals, student challenges, or what worked - Generate reflection summaries suitable for submission or self-review ### Outcome
