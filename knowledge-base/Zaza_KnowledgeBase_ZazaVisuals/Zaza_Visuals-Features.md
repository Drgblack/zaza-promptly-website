
# Zaza Visuals – Features & Capabilities

Zaza Visuals empowers teachers to generate high-quality, curriculum-aligned student feedback and classroom visuals in record time. Below is a detailed breakdown of its features and how each supports educator productivity, creativity, and consistency.

---

## 🎯 Core Features

### 1. 🔁 AI-Powered Feedback Generator
Zaza Visuals transforms brief teacher notes into fully polished student comments and visual prompts.

**How It Works**:
- Teachers input 2–3 sentences of student context.
- Select desired tone, subject area, and visual style.
- Zaza generates professional-grade output in 30 seconds or less.

**Highlights**:
- No repetitive typing—reuse and remix smart content.
- Aligned to curriculum goals and learning objectives.
- Eliminates writer's block with structured creativity.

---

### 2. 🎨 Tone & Style Controls

**Preset Tones Include**:
- Formal academic
- Friendly and upbeat
- Constructive and corrective
- Reflective and personalised
- Parent-oriented tone

**Advanced Style Adjustments**:
- Dial in warmth, assertiveness, or humility.
- Apply school- or district-specific language.
- Respect culturally diverse communication norms.
- Save personalised tone presets.

---

### 3. 📚 Curriculum-Aware Alignment

**Automatically Maps to**:
- National curriculum outcomes
- Local learning frameworks
- Subject- and grade-specific goals
- Behavioural and SEL frameworks (optional)

**Benefits**:
- Avoids generic feedback.
- Enhances transparency and consistency.
- Links comments directly to student progress and rubrics.

---

### 4. 📂 Snippet Library

A smart repository of your best-performing content.

**Features**:
- Tag, sort, and save snippets by subject, tone, or goal.
- View frequency and reuse analytics.
- Share across team (with School or Group licence).
- Import/export for backup or sharing.

---

### 5. 🌍 Multi-Language Support

Built to serve diverse classrooms and communities.

**Supported Languages**:
- English, Spanish, French, German, Italian (more to come)

**Cultural Awareness**:
- Avoids tone mismatches or unintended phrasing.
- Adjusts formality and feedback style based on locale.
- Ensures inclusivity in multilingual communication.

---

## 🚀 Advanced Features

### 6. 🧾 Bulk Feedback Generation
- Generate individualised feedback for an entire class in one go.
- Maintain nuance and tone across students.
- Export in CSV, PDF, or Google Docs.
- Compatible with report templates and grading tools.

### 7. 🧠 Custom Curriculum Mapping
- Upload internal school standards or rubrics.
- Define custom assessment tags or flags.
- Auto-apply mappings to generated outputs.
- Create specialised modules (e.g., IB, Montessori, Waldorf).

### 8. 📊 Analytics & Insights
- Track hours saved and content generated.
- Discover your most-used tones or comments.
- Audit feedback for consistency and depth.
- Share dashboards with instructional leaders.

### 9. 📤 Export Options
- PDF and DOCX formatting for reports
- CSV for spreadsheet analysis
- One-click copy for direct pasting
- SIS/LMS integrations for automated sync

---

## 🔗 Integration Ecosystem

- **Google Classroom** – Push content directly to student streams.
- **Microsoft Teams (Education)** – Integrate with assignments and chats.
- **Seesaw** – Align with student portfolios and journals.
- **Canvas/Schoology** – Sync feedback, grades, and rubrics.
- **Custom API Access** – Build internal automations or dashboards.

---

## ⚠️ Limitations (Current)

- Mobile support is limited to PWA (native apps in development).
- Custom curriculum mapping requires admin-level access.
- Snippet sharing is restricted to school/group licences.
- Currently focused on feedback and visual prompts only—lesson planning tools are part of Zaza Teach.

---

Zaza Visuals is designed to free teachers from repetitive writing tasks and help them deliver inspiring, targeted, and professional feedback with confidence.
