# Zaza Visuals – Frequently Asked Questions (Expanded & Enhanced)

Welcome to the Zaza Visuals FAQ. Below you'll find in-depth answers to the most common  -  and uncommon  -  questions educators have about using Zaza Visuals. Whether you're just starting or looking to scale its use across your school, this guide is for you.

---

## 🧠 General Questions

**Q: What exactly is Zaza Visuals, and how does it differ from tools like Canva or DALL·E?**  
A: Zaza Visuals is an AI-powered app built specifically for educators. Unlike Canva or generic AI tools, Zaza understands pedagogy, curriculum standards, age-appropriateness, and visual clarity for learning. Every visual is generated to enhance comprehension, not just aesthetics.

**Q: Is this just GPT-4 wrapped in a fancy UI?**  
A: No. Zaza Visuals uses a tightly controlled GPT-4 model paired with vision models, curriculum logic layers, and hallucination suppression systems. Outputs are intentionally constrained and filtered for educational use.

**Q: How long does it take to create a visual?**  
A: Under 10 seconds for individual visuals. For batches (e.g. 10 flashcards), it usually takes less than 30 seconds.

**Q: Is Zaza Visuals just for classroom teachers?**  
A: No. It’s used by tutors, homeschoolers, education influencers, curriculum developers, and even school communication teams.

## 🎨 Features & Functionality

**Q: What types of visuals can I generate?**  
A: Diagrams, infographics, illustrations, flashcards, video explainers, animated whiteboards, posters, classroom displays, and more  -  all optimised for pedagogy.

**Q: Can I customise the style of the visuals?**  
A: Yes. Choose from one-tap style presets (e.g. Realistic, Cartoon, Infographic) or describe the desired tone.

**Q: Can I generate visuals from voice or image input?**  
A: Yes. Speak a need (“I need a diagram of a volcano”) or snap a photo of a lesson plan to auto-generate suggestions.

**Q: Does it support curriculum alignment?**  
A: Yes. The AI considers grade level, subject, and learning outcomes. Pro users can upload standards to map visuals directly.

**Q: What if the visual isn’t quite right?**  
A: You can regenerate, edit key elements, or guide the AI with a more specific prompt.

**Q: Is there a library of saved visuals?**  
A: Yes. Your creations are saved in your dashboard. School plans include shared libraries across teachers.

**Q: Can I use Zaza Visuals on my phone or tablet?**  
A: Yes. Zaza Visuals is fully responsive on mobile and a native app is being released soon.

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

**Q: I generated a visual but it’s not showing. What do I do?**  
A: Try refreshing the browser or clearing your cache. If issues persist, email support with the prompt used and device type.

**Q: My visuals don’t look age-appropriate. Can I fix this?**  
A: Adjust the prompt to clarify the grade level. For persistent issues, contact support  -  we’re constantly improving our age filters.

**Q: The app is running slowly  -  help?**  
A: Ensure you're on a fast internet connection and a supported browser. Try disabling browser extensions if performance lags.
