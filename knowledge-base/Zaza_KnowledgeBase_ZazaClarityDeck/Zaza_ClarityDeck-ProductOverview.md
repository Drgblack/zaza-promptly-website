## Zaza ClarityDeck - Flagship Product

### Product Description
Zaza ClarityDeck is an AI-powered feedback generator that transforms how teachers create student content and parent communications. The platform reduces content writing time from 10 minutes to 30 seconds while maintaining high quality and personal touch.

### Key Value Propositions
- **Time Savings**: Reduces report writing time by up to 80%
- **Quality Assurance**: Generates curriculum-aligned, professional feedback
- **Teacher Voice**: Maintains authentic communication style through customizable tone controls
- **Zero Hallucinations**: Built with constraints to ensure factual accuracy
- **Cognitive Load Reduction**: Eliminates writer's block and mental fatigue

### Target Audience
**Primary Users:**
- Primary school teachers (ages 5-11)
- Secondary school teachers (ages 11-18)
- Teaching assistants and support staff
- Special education teachers

**Secondary Users:**
- School administrators
- Department heads
- Curriculum coordinators

### Core Problem Addressed
Teachers spend 5-10 hours per week writing student feedback and parent communications. This administrative burden contributes to teacher burnout and reduces time available for actual teaching and student interaction.
