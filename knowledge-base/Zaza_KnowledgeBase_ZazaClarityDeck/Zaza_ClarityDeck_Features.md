# Zaza ClarityDeck – Features & Functionality

Zaza ClarityDeck is your AI-powered co-pilot for writing feedback, progress summaries, and curriculum-aligned student reports. Every feature is purpose-built for time-starved teachers, supporting the emotional, administrative, and pedagogical realities of classroom life.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

### 1. AI-Powered Comment Generator
**What It Does:**  
Rapidly generates personalized student feedback using minimal input.  
**How It Works:**  
Teachers provide 2–3 sentences about a student’s progress. The AI then writes a clear, professional comment tailored to the selected tone and curriculum standards.

**Why It Matters:**  
- Eliminates blank-page anxiety and saves hours
- Maintains consistency across dozens (or hundreds) of reports
- Supports emotional nuance, even under time pressure
- Perfect for teachers juggling large class sizes or multiple subjects

---

### 2. Smart Tone & Style Controls
**What It Does:**  
Allows teachers to set the tone and formality of each comment.  
**Tone Options Include:**  
- Warm and Encouraging  
- Formal and Academic  
- Constructive and Developmental  
- Collaborative (for messages directed to parents)  
- Custom tones (e.g., based on school policies or audience culture)

**Why It Matters:**  
- Adapts to student needs and parent expectations  
- Reduces risk of miscommunication  
- Enables school-wide consistency in parent communications

---

### 3. Curriculum-Aligned Outputs
**What It Does:**  
Automatically aligns comments with national, state, or local curriculum frameworks.

**How:**  
- Draws from Zaza’s standards database for common frameworks  
- Enables uploads of custom curriculum rubrics  
- Highlights which learning goals are met

**Why It Matters:**  
- Supports evidence-based assessment  
- Reduces teacher cognitive load in matching feedback to standards  
- Helps explain student achievement to parents in understandable language

---

### 4. Personal Snippet Library
**What It Does:**  
Allows teachers to save, reuse, and tag their favourite phrases or comment structures.

**How It Works:**  
- Group snippets by subject, tone, frequency, or custom tags  
- Auto-suggests snippets during writing  
- Allows export and sharing between team members (Pro+ accounts)

**Why It Matters:**  
- Speeds up repeated writing tasks  
- Empowers teachers to maintain their authentic voice  
- Builds institutional memory over time

---

### 5. Multi-Language Support
**What It Does:**  
Supports writing feedback in multiple languages with context-aware tone adjustments.

**Languages Supported:**  
- English  
- Spanish  
- French  
- German  
- Italian  
- Other languages upon request

**Why It Matters:**  
- Empowers communication with multilingual families  
- Reflects diverse school communities  
- Maintains professionalism across language barriers

---

## 🚀 Advanced Features

### 🔁 Bulk Comment Generation
Generate reports for an entire class with just one session. Individual personalization is preserved by drawing from each student's unique context entry.

### 🎯 Custom Curriculum Mapping
Schools can upload proprietary frameworks and map feedback directly to their learning outcomes.

### 📊 Analytics Dashboard
Track usage, efficiency gains, and comment trends to support PD, quality assurance, and school leadership insights.

### 📤 Export & Integration Options
- PDF and CSV downloads  
- Direct integration with Student Information Systems (SIS)  
- One-click copy to clipboard  
- Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, and Seesaw support

---

## 🔌 Integration Capabilities

- **SIS Integration:** Sync rosters and performance data  
- **Google Classroom:** Attach comments to assignments directly  
- **Microsoft Teams / Seesaw:** Export feedback into student portfolios  
- **Open API:** For advanced or institutional integrations

---

## ⚠️ Known Limitations

- No native iOS/Android app yet (mobile-friendly web app available)  
- Snippet sharing limited to institutional licences  
- Curriculum alignment depends on accuracy of custom inputs  
- Not designed for visual content (use Zaza Visuals) or lesson generation (use Zaza Teach)

---

ClarityDeck was built in collaboration with real teachers and informed by hundreds of hours of Reddit research, classroom insights, and teacher interviews. It’s not just a tool – it’s a thoughtful solution to the daily burdens of emotional labour, admin overload, and teacher burnout.
